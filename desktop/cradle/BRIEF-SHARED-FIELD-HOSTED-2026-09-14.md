## The owner doorway (already real, do not re-implement)
`shared-field/spacetimedb/field.sh` — stdin `{kind}` → stdout `{ok,data}|{ok:false,error:{kind:unbound|unavailable|refused|malformed,message}}`.
Kinds: `status` (no network), `snapshot`, `read {ref}`, `publish {args}` (the `hostedPublicationArgs` shape), `participant`, `admit`, `contact`.
Target from env `OI_SHARED_FIELD_TARGET` (name in `hosting.json`; `frank` = the live second machine over the tailnet). Token stays under `OI_STATE_HOME`; the kernel passes env through, never reads it.
Proof it works: `printf '{"kind":"snapshot"}' | OI_SHARED_FIELD_TARGET=frank shared-field/spacetimedb/field.sh 2>/dev/null | jq .data.counts`.
