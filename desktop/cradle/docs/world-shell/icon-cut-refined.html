1 · Transport, one rack

Same slots, same drawings. The readout is what changes. Click a mode to see its words land in the rack. Click a button to arm it. Play, Stop, and Record stay the filled keys both mockups already use. Everything else is monoline, because a 19px button cannot carry a second metaphor.

2 · Modes and the two views

Session and Arrangement stay the three lanes. Four of the five mode marks stay the ones in revision 5. Base is the decision: the diamond is already an archetype, so the mode takes the die.

3 · The masks

The face is its own mesh. The wireframe mask’s polygons are traced from the drawing; each has a depth, an ink, and a colour, and can flip on its own. A small muscle map moves it: each muscle pulls a region of the mesh and inks its creases, and expressions, speech, and small involuntary movements are muscle activations. Feelings and agent states drive the rest: a flowing field on the skin around the features, panel flips, blinks, restlessness, and gestures that turn the mesh in depth. The feminine face is the same polygons moved to a second shape. mask(expr, ink, wash, size, ground, face, live); face is base or fem.

4 · Kinds and standings

One shape per kind, from the world shell’s node drawings, put on one grid. Stroke for modes, fill for these. A darker facet turns each filled shape into a solid, so it still reads at 16px. Edges keep their standing in the stroke, never in where the clip sits.

5 · Device anatomy

The Face device comes first: a live face, six pads, the muscle matrix, and the layer stack. The mouth rounds continuously from a slit into an O. Colour sits on three tessellation sizes: a slow coarse temperature over the bone, the emotion wash on the middle cells, and a finer shimmer that picks up the active muscle. Strata, wash, and bone switch that. Drag a puck to pull its muscles; Alt-drag, or press L·R, to move one side; double-click to reset. Drag a cell of the matrix up or down to push one muscle directly. The faint blue ring on each pad is where all the layers together have put the face. Below it, the rack frame from both mockups: power, name, owner, fold, pop-out, a visual, knobs, admit lamps. Folded is a title. Off is a dark lamp. The modulated knob wears a second arc so the cause stays visible. A skillset rack lists each row by its kind mark: skill or method.

6 · Clips and the needle

The session grid’s clip states, in the agent shell’s own CSS, and the arrangement needle that refuses to draw an end it does not know.

7 · Skillsets, skills, methods, methodologies

Four marks for the four kinds of carried practice. They take the sword-in-stone woodcut’s drawing, not its black masses: outlines that follow the form, short hatching where the form turns, ground under what stands. Thin line only. From 28px up the hatching shows. At 20px and below each mark drops to a reduced drawing so it still reads in a row. 16px is the browser size, 14px the device row.

8 · Field marks

Mediation for the trail, the disclosure ladder, and the strokes between bodies. Colour is the relation. Dash is its manner.

9 · World, studio, live

The die, a body and its moon, a palace plan, the Studio tools, and the Live device faces. These sit on the device frame rather than inventing a second chrome.
