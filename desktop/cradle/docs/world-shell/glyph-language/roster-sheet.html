Glyph roster

79 marks on a 24 grid, shown at 16, 20, and 24. Shell tokens are the live-shell stylesheet. Paper tokens are the document tokens from the world-shell mockup. Hover a mark: the status line reads its data-i tooltip.

InfoHover a mark. The status line is the mockup's data-i pattern: a name, then what it does.

Text and image as a chip

The frame is the shell. The ink is an engine glyph, a plain character, or an authored image. Empty engine text is drawn as O. A missed image shows waiting.
