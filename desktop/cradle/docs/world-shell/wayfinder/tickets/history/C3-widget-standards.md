## Question

Extract the mockups' component forms into the shared **guide** every device
starts from: `wayfinder/WIDGET-GUIDE.md`. Per widget: anatomy, states
(folded / compact / configure / deep / pop-out-ready), interaction (click,
keyboard, the `data-i` tooltip pattern), empty/refusing/disabled states, and
the plug-in point where a real capability attaches.

Catalogue every waiting component form: agent device, skillset rack + macros,
context-world scope, agentset rack, folded racks, hold/hand-back custody
light, steer, effort, transport takes, clip tabs, browser pool — plus the
world mockup's Revision 2-5 forms (transport re-meanings, aperture slot,
context tiles, planet-building controls).

**The guide is a floor, not a ceiling.** It exists so lanes share vocabulary
and forget nothing — not to cap how good the real feature may be. The
delivered feature is expected to function and look better than the mockup
drew it; the guide records such improvements as they land.

## Evidence / acceptance

Guide covers every device/rack/chain form in both mockups, self-described as
a floor. The landed devices (morph, colour, force, settings, the native.agent
surfaces) are mapped against it with gap lists — that inventory feeds L3.
Owner spot-checks the guide once.
