## Question

"Live sets end. Games end. Festivals end. The score remains. This is what
keeps a total environment from becoming a totalising prison" (CONT:159). "An
instrument that cannot imagine its end will defend it" (CONT:161). The DLW
already ends at the scale of the day: snapshot first, then the dated reading
(day-close.md:7-14,22). A refused proposal mutates nothing (APPB:72-76). In
the kernel an ending is a completion that hands the work back as ground: M5-5
"perfects itself by perishing into ground… its completion is the act of
handing the whole back, enriched… The cycle completes by starting" (live
Bimba map, DLW-VISION.md:186-190). A stage ends "when stage completion
criteria are met, not on fixed time intervals" (M5-5.md:19).

The one retirement the pack has prepared is the legacy cradle's: a register
of what retires, its successor and proof, the cutover gates, a migration
register, removal steps and risk notes, with the cutover "owner-named"
(LEGACY-CRADLE-RETIREMENT.md:1-8 and §1–§6). This ticket turns that shape into
a template, so that any future retirement leaves a record by the same
discipline: what retires, what does not, the named day, the gates, what is
handed back, and what supersedes it. Nothing is deleted without its record.

**It decides nothing about modes, projections or the shell** (L9). Retiring a
mode, a projection or the shell with record has no source. The template
offers the form. It names no candidate and no day.
