## 3. Process review (the owner asked)

Shape used: one Opus lead, Haiku builders (≤3, later ≤6), Sonnet for reviews
and the (refused) screen pass. About 26 builder tickets in the last stretch.

- **Correction rate.** About 9 of the 26 builder tickets needed at least one
  lead-sent correction round before landing (seam 5 rebuilt its own
  standing table instead of using `standingOf`; seam 6 put the QL adapter in
  the atlas port against a recorded decision; seam 8 opened inert windows;
  the Library relay went to the legacy `CradleFrame.tsx`; the crossing
  builder missed that the owner already consumed the law). The two Sonnet
  reviews then found 8 and 9 defects in landed Haiku work, including two
  high-severity ones (a restored workspace could come back empty; the kernel
  could write any `.als` non-atomically).
- **Where Haiku held up:** mechanical, well-bounded tickets — registers,
  conformance pins, lockfiles, test fixes, plumbing with a named seam, the
  register's evidence pass.
- **Where it did not:** anything needing design judgment or reading intent —
  which store is the one store, which module is the owner, what a mockup
  means, whether a body stands alone, which file is product. Haiku follows
  the brief literally; when the brief is loose (mine, "find the listener"),
  it lands in the wrong place confidently.
- **Cost and speed.** Haiku builders ran 6–30 minutes and roughly 150k–390k
  tokens each (~6–7M across the stretch); Sonnet reviews ~245k–420k each.
  The rework rounds roughly doubled the cost of the tickets that needed
  them, and the review findings cost a further wave.
- **Recommendation for the next legs:**
  1. Sonnet builders for UI, device-SDK and anything mockup-bound; Haiku only
     for mechanical tickets with an exact seam and a test to satisfy.
  2. Every UI ticket returns screenshots from a computer-use pass of the
     native candidate (or the ticket is not done) — once access works.
  3. Briefs name the product target and the owning module explicitly
     (`BRIEF-BUILDER.md` Target block; `candidate-reachable.mjs`), and cite
     the mockup region and the manual section the UI must match.
  4. Keep the Sonnet review per wave; it paid for itself.
