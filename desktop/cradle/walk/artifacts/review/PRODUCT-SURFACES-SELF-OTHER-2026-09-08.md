## What the adopted experience requires

Self and Other frame the whole experience: personal O:I and the shared field of other worlds are regions of one navigable field. They are situated relations over existing Participants, not additional identity/account types. Selected openness must be explicit. The September 8 owner-directed addendum supersedes the earlier interpretation of Self/Other as only a tiny initial demonstration. [Owner UX](../../../../../.superpowers/sdd/cradle-rebuild/SELF-OTHER-FIELD-UX-2026-09-08.md), [architecture: Shared Field, Self toward Other](../../../../../docs/SHARED-FIELD.md#the-parent-01-relation--self-toward-other).

The optional right sidebar remains the person's accompanying agent. Shared node/profile/NOW details belong in ordinary canvas panes, full views or native popouts; they do not displace the agent or create another permanent sidebar. The subject's local tree/constellation and its details can sit beside one another inside that surface arrangement. Re-dock preserves the same reference and returns the tab; no new semantic object or copied draft is created. [Owner pane contract](../../../../../.superpowers/sdd/cradle-rebuild/SELF-OTHER-FIELD-UX-2026-09-08.md#one-pane-system-an-optional-accompanying-agent).

## Inspected implementation and drift

- Current canvas dispatch explicitly mounts encounter, file, System, knowledge and source bodies, plus the source-list path. This is a finite implemented set, not yet the full contribution registry promised by the application spec. [Workbench](../../../src/surface/Workbench.tsx), [native detached host](../../../src/workspace/DetachedFrame.tsx).
- `selfOtherReadModel({self, others, field})` validates Participants against a field and retains their identities. It is a usable existing semantic seam; adding a desktop-owned Self/Other identity model would duplicate it. [social.mjs](../../../../../shared-field/social.mjs).
- `createExploreSurfaceModel()` composes existing search/open/read/relations/sources/explain and WorldPresentation/Projection readings. Its seed must come from the authorised live provider; rendering a static seed does not establish hosted participation or admission. [Explore model](../../../../../shared-field/explore-surface.mjs), [transport lifecycle](../../../../../shared-field/transport-lifecycle.mjs).

This is source evidence, not a fresh live-provider result. Do not carry the public-only limitation forward as current implementation truth, and do not infer a shared-NOW lifecycle from general private content support. [current server implementation](../../../../../shared-field/spacetimedb/src/index.ts), [private-content receipt](../../../../../shared-field/PHASE1-PRIVATE-CONTENT-SECURITY-RECEIPT.md), [older README](../../../../../shared-field/README.md).

## Bounded presentation recommendation — inference, not new product authority

Keep one root navigation grammar capable of local Central and the admitted Shared Field, preserving each scope and its availability. Opening either binds an ordinary canvas subject. The global top bar continues to operate on the selected pane/subject; it is not a second social navigation controller. Preserve the optional companion on the right while shared details open beside a constellation in canvas panes.

The first real shared vertical should consume the existing field-relative Participant and Explore application seams, resolve a real selected Self and Other, and open one admitted world/material object with provenance and audience intact. Expose only owner-disclosed operations. This establishes useful placement without inventing a feed, account system, membership authority, automatic publishing or substitute NOW product. Shared NOW and rich publication follow their actual owner integrations; independent local pane work continues meanwhile.

No production code, source documentation, provider state or user data was changed by this recovery. No visual or hosted-provider acceptance is asserted.
