## 0. Owner clarification this Wayfinder locks

There is one Expressions environment with two operating readings:

```text
                   ONE EXPRESSIONS ENVIRONMENT
                native subjects and source identities
                source-bound working constructions
                existing composition / Scene system
                existing Stage per native window
                              │
              ┌───────────────┴───────────────┐

        3:3 lived cut                     4:2 deep cut
        Nara / Anima                      Epii / Technē
```

The two modes are not the same distinction as connected source material versus authored constellation organisation. Technē is creative, operates through Expressions and can show ordinary source material; Expressions can disclose sources and authored constellations. Neither mode owns a separate semantic world.

Technē is not six applications, six mandatory tabs, a launcher, a second graph/store/runtime, or a set of waiting panes. Switching mode changes tools and disclosure while preserving the selected work and native identities. Nara and Epii retain their actual distinct canonical Agent/Agency/session relations: continuity is not identity conflation or a new chat per lens.

### Explicit supersession of restrictive earlier wording

1. The earlier host/HUD-only scope and instruction not to repair instrument internals are superseded. Preserve native ownership and useful K/AW/TB0/refinement code; repair incomplete creation, persistence, selection, engine binding and Return inside their actual owners when necessary.

3. Project overview plus one scene per constellation is an initial navigation grammar, not identity equality. One constellation may have multiple scenes/presentations; one scene may contain multiple constellations; a source may occur in several constructions.

5. A renderer budget or small conversion helper is not the product boundary. Source identities and the full authored whole must survive bounded rendering.
6. The correct Epii default, Central-rooted graph navigator, real Library, full-screen Technē controls and source portals remain mandatory. The constructive clarification adds their operational substance; it does not replace them.
