## 0. Launch principle

Every session starts in its **native repository**, reads its native issue/doc first, then this map only for cross-product relation.

No worker may:

- move source authority into O:I;
- move generic Expression semantics into Point-Cloud;
- move QL meaning into AIKit/Actuation;
- move provider/session identity into Nara identity;
- move corpus ontology into the renderer;
- patch another product locally to get around a missing seam;
- claim lived/runtime acceptance from a document or mocked adapter.

# 11. Human verification points

Human judgement is explicitly required for:

- whether an Expression carries the intended source burden;
- whether associative colour/form use works or misleads;
- whole-Mytheme narrative adequacy;
- whether corpus continuity has become monotonous or incoherent;
- whether a returned practice deserves Recognition;
- final lived UX and submission acceptance.

Automation may prepare evidence and comparison. It does not replace those judgements.
