## 0. Undertaking and first delivery

Complete the existing Flow so one human and several actual agents can converse, branch, converge and retain useful work in one NOW, then perform the same activity across Workcells and independently grounded worlds. A Flow stays rich, portable source; native journals carry contact/runtime effects; different participants keep their own sessions and contexts.

The first installed delivery is a real passage-to-explanation/Expression inquiry:

1. Open a real source and continuing Flow; bring two distinct agents into the concern.
2. Request complementary work: recover the argument and test an example. Both return independently with their sources and actual identities.
3. Develop a side inquiry and one contribution that relates both answers. Change a material assumption while an answer is still running.
4. Close the Flow/UI before the delayed response arrives. Reopen and find every response on its correct request and basis, once.
5. Continue with a fresh body across a civil Day boundary; use the retained result on a different example.
6. Repeat across Mac/Omarchy, then admit a second independently authorised person/test identity and their agent through SharedField. Keep private neighbouring source out of its actual delivered context.

The first local episode may proceed before the remote/connector work. It is an early useful increment, not closure of the whole scope.
