## 1. Decisions developers must see before the work packages

**The Wiki is first-class connected writing.** Implement Markdown links, wikilinks, backlinks, tags, exact passages, strong navigation and search without mandatory QL. Reader and graph share native link evidence. The small-node/thin-line Wiki basis remains.

**Constellations are made, not just displayed.** Support material-first discovery and frame-first construction, contextual roles, editable connections, modular reuse and variants. A chosen QL frame may be an explicit provisional interpretation. Shape validity does not prove semantic truth.

**Technè is the real Expression medium.** Its target includes selectable glyphs, 3D arrangement, text writing, images, lines and particle relations, physical material, actual Scenes and Palace compositions. Do not substitute a flat graph converter or pre-generated scene files.

**The agent staging space is shared constructive work.** The current question, selected sources, frame, proposed roles/relations, human edits and outputs are structured native work over the same corpus. Existing Agency/session/context and `TechneReading`/`DisclosureSession`/Expression operations carry it.

**The results return.** Native saved constellations, deliberately authored QL relations and new artifacts enter ordinary Wiki/Library discovery with provenance. Whole/member structure survives. Visual connections become semantic only through deliberate authoring, not coordinate inference.

**Use the kernel's retained resource layer.** Native ownership does not prohibit caches. Source readings, adjacency, in-flight work and working models persist above disposable views through the existing continuity architecture. The earlier no-cache statement and ten-item converter are historical implementation limits to reconcile, not requirements to preserve.

The source/constellation distinction, the Expressions/Technè application cuts and the three state responsibilities are independent. All fifteen WC decisions and reasons remain in the product record, not hidden in transport types.

## 6. First vertical and dependency order

Start with one ordinary Markdown collection, including duplicate titles, a heading link, a wikilink, a tag, an unresolved link, media and a permitted exact passage. WC-W1/W2 and WC-T1/T2 can proceed in parallel on their agreed boundary. WC-W3 improves navigation independently. Join WC-W4 with WC-T3, then WC-W5 with WC-T5. Extend the same objects through WC-T4 and complete WC-W6.

The joined specimen: read → follow backlink → select passages → create a frame-first or material-first constellation → human and agent revise it → express glyphs/relations/text/images in 3D → save Scenes and Palace → return a typed QL relation and new artifact → find both in Wiki → mode/restart return → source-change review. A second constellation reuses one source with a different role. The ordinary Wiki also works with QL absent.

No dependency on finishing all corpus publication or a hosted service is introduced. Native authoring limitations are implemented, not demoted to permanent view-only behaviour.
