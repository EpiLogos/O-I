# {O:I}

**Operating Infrastructure · Objective Internality**

{O:I} names and develops the technological field in which model capacity becomes situated agency.

A capable model is not yet a capable agent in a particular world. What it can do depends on the world around it: what persists between invocations, what can be reached, which powers and sources are available, how agency is constituted and bounded, what environments can be made real, and how evidence and difference return to the people and systems that bear the consequences.

We build and research that surrounding field so it can be developed, compared, selectively composed, and selectively shared.

## Capacity and agency

Model capacity and situated agency are different variables. The surrounding world is itself an engineering field. **Provisioning** makes conditions for action available; **potentiation** changes what can become actual through them. Many consequential changes to an agentic system happen without changing model weights.

We ask: given available model capacity, what technological structures make particular forms of agency possible, and what changes when those structures change?

The smallest useful case and the richest research case are points in one possibility space:

```text
persistent authored ground
        +
actuated model capacity
        ↓
a situated technological agency
```

That relation can remain small. It can also open into explicit agency composition, broader capability and knowledge horizons, durable development, material execution worlds, shared fields, and formal research. We enter relation with the worlds people already have — directories, CLIs, existing tools, agent runtimes, machines, arrangements we did not create — without requiring migration into one canonical stack. Existing arrangements are practical realities to preserve and research facts about the field.

## What we build, and for whom

The human/agent relation leads. A person should be able to write who they are, what they care about, how agents should work with them, and what they refuse — in their own words, as durable source that becomes selectively operative in later agency, instead of re-typing themselves into every prompt.

**Intent preservation** is the hard problem. Implementation can move much faster than human meaning. An agent can convert a phrase into a hierarchy of types, tickets, tests, and interfaces within hours. Each transformation can be locally competent while gradually deleting the reason the phrase mattered. The resulting system may preserve every noun and still lose the proposition. Authored intent and ordinary work must survive the lifecycle of whichever agent product is current — purpose is not forfeited to tooling churn.

Human agency is the success condition. The aim is not merely to automate enough mechanics that a person gets some time back. It is to place human authorship where it has the greatest consequence for the form of the resulting agency. High-leverage contact points:

- **Authorship** — writing purpose, principles, preferences, and positions in one's own voice, so they remain available as durable ground rather than vanishing into a prompt history.
- **Authority** — deciding what an Agent may do, on whose behalf, and within what bounds.
- **Commission** — determining that a consequential line of work is worth undertaking and why.
- **Recognition** — encountering what development or reality has returned and deciding what it means.
- **Revision** — changing the ground, the bounds, or the commission when the returned world shows that the earlier determination no longer holds.
- **Refusal and redirection** — declining a path, withholding acceptance, or sending the work elsewhere, as ordinary constitutional acts rather than exceptional failure modes.

**Return** is part of the design — evidence, resistance and difference come back to the locus that governs what happens next. Delegated or determining authority can become separated from the locus that actually meets resistance, error, unforeseen possibility and consequence. If evidence and difference have no path back into the governing locus, command becomes insulated from the reality produced under it.

We judge the arrangement by more than labour saved: does greater technological agency increase the person's ability to author the structures that matter, reduce the need to repeat or police those structures mechanically, and return reality in a form from which the person can genuinely revise them?

## The composition circuit

The products exist to carry one relation through time:

```text
human writes / chooses / determines
            ↓
durable authored ground + authority
            ↓
relevant parts become operative for an act (disclosure)
            ↓
agent exercises substantial situated judgement
            ↓
world resists, answers, surprises or confirms
            ↓
evidence and difference Return
            ↓
human recognises, refuses, redirects or revises
```

This is a different design problem from keeping a human inside every loop iteration. Continuous approval can sometimes be necessary for risk, authority or safety, but it is not the general model of human agency we are pursuing. The deeper question is where the person should touch the system so that their authorship continues to matter after the immediate act of writing or deciding has ended.

The six centres are not a product catalogue. They are custodians of stretches of this circuit:

- **Central** holds the ground — authored source that remains recognisably a person's own across changing models, interfaces, machines and runtimes.
- **Actuation** constitutes the act and admits its return — how Agency is determined, bounded, composed, and how attributable difference comes back. The centre is custodian of the actuation relation defined below.
- **AIKit** forms the operative horizon — making models, capabilities, Actions, sources, sessions, runtime bodies and Surfaces available here and now without rewriting them into one runtime.
- **Software Factory** keeps developmental continuity — authored intention through design, implementation, evidence, candidate realities, Recognition and Return as software changes.
- **Workcell** materialises computational worlds — workspaces, processes, services, machines and networks with inspectable lifecycle and evidence.
- **Quaternal Logic** holds the formal and experimental horizon — where archetypal and relational form is made technically answerable under the discipline of operational consequence.

{O:I} itself is the Idea and whole relating these centres. It is not a seventh product beside them.

## What an explicit World makes possible

A **World** is the working environment defined for an agent: the projects, sources, capabilities, history, authority, material environment and relations through which it can act. Agency is constituted through the relation between available inference capacity and such a World.

Ever more explicit definition of World means a world can be validated, made visible, and tested. It can be inspected rather than assumed, compared rather than debated, changed deliberately rather than drifted into.

The world, not the chat transcript, is the durable object of human–AI interaction, because world determines what output and action are possible at all. Chat is an encounter surface. The structures that persist, constrain, disclose and return are what make successive encounters continuous with one another and answerable to the person who authored the ground. How we hold capacity constant and vary the surrounding arrangement is the research stance in Horizon below.

## The primitives

The primitives are structure, not incantation. They walk the composition circuit: ground the person authors, the actor who stands in that ground, the act that determines and bounds agency, what persists and discloses across acts, how claims stay distinguishable when reality returns, how worlds meet, what materialises, and the formal horizon of the research.

### Ground

Without durable ground, every act begins by re-inventing the person.

A **World**, as above, is the working environment defined for an agent.

**Authored ground** is the durable human-authored material through which a person's technological world can remain recognisably theirs: purpose, principles, preferences, rules, ways of working, project positions and other durable statements written in the person's own words, becoming selectively operative in later agency.

A **Project** is a bounded unit of that ground and work — a place where purpose, sources, history and development cohere so that agency has somewhere to stand and something to answer to.

Human-authored source, observed state and agent-generated interpretation are different kinds of thing. An agent may observe a recurring behaviour, infer a preference, summarise a long document, or propose a new rule. Those can be useful derived objects. They should not silently rewrite what the human authored. Where a generated proposal ought to become durable authored ground, Recognition (the contact point above) or another explicit acceptance act closes that transition.

### Actor

Without a stable actor, a new model or harness looks like a new being.

An **Agent** is an enduring semantic identity — who or what may act across sessions, models and runtimes.

**Agency** is that Agent situated for an act: relative to a world, a role, authority, and stance. The same Agent may occupy different Agencies as situation changes.

**AgentSession**, **Execution** and **Harness** are replaceable runtime constitution. Changing a model, process or harness does not by itself create a new enduring Agent. Identity and situation remain distinguishable from the particular machinery that carries an act this time.

### Act

Without determination and bounds, situated judgement has no attributable shape — and Return has nowhere to land.

**Determination** is how situated Agency takes shape for an act: including how it articulates loci, delegates under attributable bounds, derives new loci with provenance, or federates without collapsing identities. The four relations and their contracts live with the Actuation centre and architecture docs.

**Authority** and **bounds** say what an Agent may do, on whose behalf, and where action must stop. **Refusal** is a constitutional relation in that field, not an error path.

A **labouring encounter** is the contact between Agency and a resisting world — where judgement meets material that answers, surprises, or confirms.

**Actuation** — the primitive relation — is the whole-preserving relation by which a situated Agency determines a bounded plurality of agentic loci, governs how those loci may operate and interrelate, and admits their returned difference into the world from which the determination arose. The product centre [Actuation](https://github.com/EpiLogos/Actuation) is custodian of this relation: it develops and governs how the relation is constituted in practice. Metagency, RootAgency, and AgenticComposition deepen that custody; they are taught there, not on this front door.

### Across acts

Without continuity across invocations, each act is orphaned from the last — and disclosure and Return have nothing durable to carry.

**Objective Internality** names the objectively inspectable structures that can become part of an actor's operative interior across acts. These structures can be inspected and changed independently of a particular model invocation, yet they can also be disclosed into that invocation and become part of what the actor can recognise, understand, and do. Three dimensions matter to us in the present synthesis:

- **Continuity** — what persists and can return across acts: Projects, histories, memories, decisions, identities, authored positions.
- **Potency** — what can become causally available: knowledge, capabilities, tools, authority, runtime bodies and material environments.
- **Authorship and orientation** — what gives the operative field purpose, priority, judgement, source authority, limits and reasons for revision, including structures deliberately authored by humans.

The claim is operational rather than a declaration about phenomenal consciousness.

**Disclosure** makes relevant structure available to an actor for an act without rewriting heterogeneous sources into one runtime. It is how authored ground and other world-structure become operative rather than merely stored — the circuit step between durable ground and situated judgement.

**Return** brings evidence, resistance and attributable difference back to the locus that can recognise, refuse, redirect or revise. Without Return, the circuit breaks: determination loses contact with the world it produced.

### Claims

When reality returns, kinds of claim must stay distinguishable — or generated material silently becomes the person's ground.

The **standing ladder** keeps kinds of claim distinct:

```text
authored position
    why this is worth doing and what must not be lost

design commitment
    what the product is intended to become and which distinctions are deliberate

architecture contract
    how the present system must remain true in form

implementation fact
    what is actually implemented

observed evidence
    dated receipts for present-tense claims

agent inference
    derived readings that have not been accepted as authored ground
```

**Provenance** marks who authored or maintains a claim, and what kind of thing it is. Without it, generated material and human source blur.

**Evidence** is dated receipt: observations, tests, run results and returned difference that support present-tense claims without silently promoting them to position or design.

Commission and Recognition remain the contact points taught above: they are how consequential work is opened under purpose, and how returned reality is judged — including whether a proposal may enter authored ground.

### Between worlds

People and agents already live in more than one world; the field must say how those worlds meet without erasing their grounds.

Independently grounded worlds can enter relation without ceasing to be separately grounded.

**Projection** makes a representation available to another human, Agent or world. It does not transfer canonical ownership or mutation authority over the source. Mechanics of that relation live behind the shared-field docs linked below.

A **SharedField** is the mediated space in which projected material can be encountered. An **Encounter** is the contact through which participants meet that material and produce attributable difference.

**Objective Co-Internality** names the objective relation in which independently grounded operative worlds become mutually implicated while retaining their difference. Self and Other are situated positions, not identity kinds. The fuller shared grammar is taught in [`docs/SHARED-FIELD.md`](docs/SHARED-FIELD.md) and [`docs/OBJECTIVE-CO-INTERNALITY.md`](docs/OBJECTIVE-CO-INTERNALITY.md).

### Material

Agency needs computational bodies; those bodies must not become the ontology of World or Act.

Material structure — workspaces, processes, services, containers, hosts, databases and other computational worlds — acquires explicit **placement**, **lifecycle**, **bindings** and **observed evidence**. That embodiment is real and inspectable. It does not become the higher-level ontology of World, Agency or Act; it is what those higher relations may demand and what Workcell materialises.

### Horizon

**Quaternal Logic / MEF**, under Quaternal Logic as custodian, is the formal and experimental horizon of the wider Epi-Logos programme. The software discipline is **operational consequence**: if a distinction is supposed to matter to an operation, the implementation should eventually make a discriminable difference that can be tested rather than merely carrying the terminology. Software results answer questions about the technical articulation of the research. They do not by themselves prove the metaphysics from which the research was motivated.

We place epistemic cultivation here: deepening what can be known and compared about agency under controlled variation. When model capacity is held constant, the surrounding arrangement becomes the independent variable. Legitimate research outcomes include a simpler arrangement beating a complex one, and formal difference with operational equivalence. The value is comparability — making enough of the surrounding structure explicit that those outcomes can be seen without collapsing everything back into an undifferentiated claim about model capability.

## The six centres

The present family has six native product centres — centres of responsibility and custodians of stretches of the composition circuit. Each stands on its own and meets heterogeneous alternatives through explicit seams.

| Centre | What it is | What it changes |
|---|---|---|
| [**Central**](https://github.com/EpiLogos/Central) | Durable human-authored ground: the source through which a person's technological world can remain recognisably theirs across changing models, interfaces, machines and runtimes. | Authored intent and ordinary work do not have to be surrendered to the lifecycle of whichever agent product is current. |
| [**Actuation**](https://github.com/EpiLogos/Actuation) | Custodian of the actuation relation: constitution and governance of situated agency — identity, Agency, determination, authority, bounds, labouring encounter, attributable difference and Return. | A plurality of agents can be related without reducing agency to process orchestration or hiding who may determine, refuse, observe and learn from the act. |
| [**AIKit**](https://github.com/EpiLogos/ai-kit) | Operative composition and disclosure: making a heterogeneous field of models, capabilities, Actions, sources, sessions, runtime bodies, environments and Surfaces available to an actor without rewriting them into one runtime. | Models, skills, Actions, sources, sessions, runtime bodies and Surfaces can form a usable horizon without requiring all of them to be rewritten into one agent runtime. |
| [**Software Factory**](https://github.com/EpiLogos/agent-system-design) | Developmental continuity: keeping authored intention, design, implementation, evidence, candidate realities, Recognition and Return in intelligible relation as software changes. | Fast agentic implementation can remain answerable to human purpose instead of preserving technical nouns while silently losing why the software was worth making. |
| [**Workcell**](https://github.com/EpiLogos/Workcell) | Technological embodiment: turning provider-neutral material demand into actual workspaces, processes, services, machines, networks and other computational worlds with inspectable lifecycle and evidence. | Workspaces, processes, services, containers, VMs, hosts, databases and browser-accessible applications acquire explicit placement, lifecycle, bindings and observed evidence without becoming the higher-level ontology. |
| [**Quaternal Logic**](https://github.com/EpiLogos/QL-MEF) | Custodian of the formal and experimental horizon: inquiry into QL/MEF structures and their operational consequences, including deeper Epi-Logos research where those structures are relevant. | See Horizon above — operational consequence and comparability, not metaphysical proof by software. |

A real act can touch two centres or all six, enter at different points, substitute another technology, or never require one of them at all. Composition is relation, not compulsory adoption.

## The `oi` front door

`oi` is the shared doorway into the composed system. It discloses what is installed and routes a human or Agent toward the native command and documentation surfaces without making the native products inaccessible on their own.

```text
oi central ...      -> ctrl         (compatibility alias: oi ctrl)
oi actuation ...    -> actuation
oi aikit ...        -> aikit         (compatibility alias: oi kit)
oi factory ...      -> factory
oi workcell ...     -> workcell
oi ql ...           -> ql
oi products [--json]
```

The command namespace is composition convenience, not semantic ownership. All six product commands are declared `"command_standing": "accepted-main"` in `surfaces.json`. Source-level standing does not imply physical #97 acceptance; see `suite/mainline.json` for the standing qualifications.

## Operating Infrastructure

The acronym holds both poles of one field: **Operating Infrastructure** is the engineering face; **Objective Internality** is the same field from the actor's side.

Operating Infrastructure is the engineering around model inference through which an artificial actor can operate in a durable world: projects and authored ground, contextual and epistemic horizons, capabilities and Actions, sessions and runtime bodies, development history, material environments, and relations to other actors and worlds. An ordinary operating system gives programs files, processes, memory, devices and permissions. Agentic systems need another level of organisation around model invocation. Many of these functions already exist in separate tools; we supply a common field in which their distinct responsibilities and relations can be made explicit.
