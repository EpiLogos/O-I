# M-prime development — the domain loop

Use this Skill for any work in the M-prime domain: the world shell and its
frame, the device SDK and its families, the racks, projections, mode
system, instruments and documents. This is the ecology-of-law method for
the domain — the founding text is [FOUNDING.md](FOUNDING.md) beside this
file; read it once, work from here.

## The loop (each step has its command; skip nothing)

1. **Orient in the law.** Read the design sections your work touches, the
   spine (is this a T-thread? the spine decides where it lands), the
   ownership map (whose family), and the editor standard (what craft bar).
   Consult the authored ground before any code — code is a poor witness of
   why a thing exists. Read the figure's referent (06 §0): the four modes
   are chambers of one instrument; Epi-Logos is the philosophical corpus —
   the esoteric identity projected over the exoteric substrate as the
   epi-logos mode — and this domain builds the instrument it is played on.
   A thread is right when the day-arc of 06 §7 can be performed through
   what it lands.

2. **Claim the lane.** `seat join env-1 o-i --new-lane --actor <you>
   --region <your paths>` — or attach as co-worker without `--new-lane`.
   Never over uncommitted work; the register holds the claim.

3. **Find the work.** `npm run devices:gaps` (capability matrices joined
   with the admitted surface), or a spine thread, or the owner's word.
   The gap between a capability's need and the family's declared surface
   is the work candidate.

4. **Declare to the law.** `npm run devices:scaffold -- <family> <owner>
   <device> <title> [icon]`, then declare faces and §14 rows. The gate
   refuses dishonesty at admission and names each fault: write paths must
   be membered (kernel ops, shell setters with authority, known tools),
   modes and formats must be from the ontology, a product binding must be
   registered or claimed with authority, a waiting face carries nothing.

5. **Build with the kit.** Compose faces from the kit's controls against
   the proven aperture; marks from the cut only (`npm run
   devices:sync-icons -- --check`); tokens only, never raw colours; a
   modulated row names its modulator and shows it.

6. **Prove to the tiers.** `npm run test:device-sdk` (kit tests + world
   gate), `npm run devices:walk` (scripted browser capture), `npm run
   devices:validate`, and the build. Green here is the floor, not the
   acceptance.

7. **Break it yourself.** Dispatch a FRESH adversarial subagent against
   the work — new escapes, doc-verbatim followability, specimen parity,
   law-coverage holes — and fix to its SHIP verdict. Then a conduct audit:
   every "known limit" either closed or named genuinely-external with its
   owner, in plain words. Open tails are the ecology's poison.

8. **Land.** `node gates/run.mjs landing` at your tip (green receipt, the
   definition of tested), push the branch, open the PR, merge by the
   repo's law. Escape hatches (no-verify, skip) are owner-asks: named,
   rare, recorded in the PR and the return.

9. **Return plainly.** NOW return for the agents (the register's own
   grammar); plain speech to the owner. Both truthful about what is
   actual, what waits, and on whom.

## Founding forward

A new domain (a recurring kind of work with scattering knowledge) is
founded by the blueprint in [FOUNDING.md](FOUNDING.md): name the specimen,
name the law, make the law checkable, make the loop repeatable, record
the method from a completed arc, land it, evolve it as one movement. The
M-prime domain is the worked example; found the next one from it, not
from memory.

## Not-do laws (binding)

- No bespoke UI, no second admission path, no second registry, no new icon
  vocabulary outside the cut, no raw colours in faces.
- No writePath without its writer; no illuminated lamp on an unknown
  state; no claim of actuality without executed evidence.
- No open tails: close what can be closed from your seat; name the rest
  with its owner; never dress one as the other.
- No method founded speculatively — a method is recorded from a completed
  arc, with the receipts.
- No landing without the gate; no gate bypass without the owner's word,
  recorded.
- A family appears in exactly four places — browser, rack, projections,
  inspectors — plus time bindings and telemetry.
