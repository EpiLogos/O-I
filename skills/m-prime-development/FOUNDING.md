# The founding of an ecology of law — M-prime development as the worked example

*Founded 10 October 2026, by `agent:zcode-mprime-founding`, at the owner's
commission. This document states the pattern once, from the domain that
proved it. Every later domain founds itself the same way and points back
here as the blueprint.*

## What an ecology of law is

A development domain is not a folder of work. It is an **ecology of law**:
a small number of living elements that hold the domain's **form** (its
order, what it can be) and drive its **actuality** (what is actually being
made true) as one field. The elements:

| Element | Holds | Test of life |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The specimen** | the domain's form, cut and owned by a person | a human can read it and point at it |
| **The design law** | the named order — projections, registries, grammars, surfaces | the law names what is forbidden as precisely as what is required |
| **The standard** | the craft bar — how the made thing must feel and behave | a maker can be refused by it |
| **The registry** | the made checkable — generated truth, drift-locked | the test that regenerates it exists |
| **The gate** | law made executable | it refuses, at admission time, with the fault named |
| **The tool** | praxis made repeatable | one command runs the loop's step |
| **The method** | the memory of the movement — this document's siblings | a newcomer can run the domain from it |
| **The register** | the truthful state — NOW, receipts, returns | an agent reads it and is not misled |

The elements are one **field of logos** because each is the others in
another mode: the gate is law made executable; the registry is law made
regenerable; the tool is praxis made repeatable; the method is the memory
of the movement between them. Remove any element and the ecology degrades
in a characteristic way — law without gates becomes opinion; gates without
a specimen become bureaucracy; tools without method become archaeology;
method without a lived instance becomes speculation.

## Inpotential and actuality — the honesty law of the ecology

Aristotle's distinction, operationalised. The domain's law is
**inpotential** (dynamis): declared form, able to be actual. Work is
**actuality** (energeia): executed, evidenced, landed. The ecology has
exactly two honest states for any item of work — **actual** (with executed
evidence) or **inpotential with a named owner and cause** (declared,
waiting on something real). The dishonest third state — declared done, or
left dangling without an owner — is the ecology's one poison. It was the
founder's own measured fault: three rounds of "known limits" that were
closable all along, until the review named them open tails. The law that
came out of it: **close what can be closed from your seat; name what
cannot, in plain words, with its owner; never dress either as the other.**

## The worked example — what this domain founded, in order

The M-prime shell domain (the world shell, its SDK, its devices) founded
its ecology like this:

1. **Specimen**: `icon-cut.html` — the owner's cut of the icon language,
   the mode marks, the transport slots. One file, human-readable, owned.
2. **Design law**: `WORLD-SHELL-DESIGN.md` (§12–16: families, the uncarved
   block, §14 addresses, time strata, four surfaces) + the spine docs.
3. **Standards**: `NATIVE-EDITOR-STANDARD.md` (the editor craft),
   `AGENT-DEVICE-OWNERSHIP.md` (the verified ownership map).
4. **Registries**: `icons.ts` (generated from the specimen, drift-locked),
   `kernelOps.ts` (generated from the kernel's types, drift-locked),
   `AGENT_SHELL_ADDRESS_TABLE` (the verified map, cross-checked).
5. **The gate**: `validate.ts` + `scripts/validate-devices.mjs` — refuses
   dishonest declarations at admission, and re-checks the whole world.
6. **The tools**: `devices:gaps` (find the work), `devices:scaffold`
   (begin it honestly), `devices:validate`, `devices:walk` (prove it),
   `devices:dev` (see it live).
7. **The methods**: `skills/device-sdk/SKILL.md` (the device loop) and
   `skills/m-prime-development/SKILL.md` (the domain loop — this ecology's
   own method).
8. **The register**: the NOW returns and the landing receipts.

## Founding a new ecology (the blueprint)

When a new domain emerges — its work recurring, its knowledge scattering:

1. **Name the specimen** — the one human-readable source that owns the
   domain's form. If none exists, the domain is not ready to be founded;
   make the specimen first.
2. **Name the law** — write the design law until it names refusals as
   precisely as requirements.
3. **Make each piece of law checkable** — a gate, a registry with a drift
   lock, or a test. Law that cannot be checked is aspiration; say so until
   it can be made checkable.
4. **Make the loop repeatable** — one command per step of the work.
5. **Record the method** — the SKILL.md, written from a completed arc,
   never speculatively (see the Hermes finding: work without a method is
   archaeology for the next agent).
6. **Land it** — through the gates, to main, and return to the register.
7. **Evolve by the same movement** — a change to the law lands with its
   gate and its method updated in the same commit. A law that moved without
   its gate is the poison state again.
