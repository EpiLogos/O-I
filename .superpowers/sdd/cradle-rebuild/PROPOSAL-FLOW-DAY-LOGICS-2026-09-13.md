## 1. What a Flow is

A Flow is a view over entries of your Day carrier — not a file. The 0/1
document (`ql-dialogue-flow_1.html`) already holds this: Flow and Dialogue are
two stances over the same document entries (Flow opens an entry as a full
writing page; Dialogue reads the same entry set conversationally), and Journal
is a second collection inside that same file. There is no per-flow Markdown
source anywhere in the design. "Starting a flow" means adding an entry to
your carrier; "opening a flow" means opening that carrier at an entry.

## 2. How Start writing / New flow resolve

Into your Day, through your own document operations:

- Writing that is a Day goes to the 4+2 die (`ql-daily-die_2.html`) as the
  Day: Central's source/temporal reading names today's carrier, and opening a
  tab or a writing surface never mints another Day.
- Flow/Dialogue writing adds an entry to the 0/1 document. A blank writing
  surface is legitimate; it is local until you save it into the carrier.
- No writing surface, tab, or agent allocates a Day, a NOW, or a file as a
  side effect of opening.

Today `flow_create/flow_read/flow_write/flow_inspect/flow_list` address the
per-flow `.md` store. Under this position they re-aim at the carrier: entry
operations over the Day/0/1 documents through your document family, so one
document holds the entries and every view reads it. The standalone store and

entries are carried over under the same ratification.
