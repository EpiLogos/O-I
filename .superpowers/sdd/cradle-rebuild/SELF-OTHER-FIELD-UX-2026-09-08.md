Standing: owner-directed UX specification and execution addendum, September 8.
The owner's latest clarification supersedes the earlier agent interpretation of
Self/Other as only an initial proof and NOW as a separate activity reading.
Product availability below is audited source, never inferred from desired UX.
The latest owner decisions on pane placement, NOW basis and keeping selected

## Begin with the person

I have my O:I. It contains my pages, projects, experiments, knowledge and agents.
I can make a page, an HTML demo, an image, a document or another canvas object,
give it a wiki page and a description, and connect it to the rest of my work.

When I want to share it, I choose how much of that world to open. Someone can
try the demo, read what I was attempting and follow it back to me. If I choose,
they can also inspect the agents, Skills, harness and profiles involved. I can
show a useful way of working, including what shaped it, as well as its output.

I can put a question or experiment into the field and invite people and agents
to join. It can be public, private, lasting or temporary. The work remains tied
back to the worlds from which it came. I can follow somebody else's work in the
same way, discover a related idea or agent, and move into a new constellation.

Self and Other frame this entire experience: my personal O:I and the shared
field of other worlds are regions of one navigable field. My chosen openness
determines which parts of my world are available to whom. The interface should
make that openness understandable at the point of sharing and participation;
it should not make ordinary exploration feel like database administration.

## Make a page and let someone experience it

The canvas presents the actual work: the running HTML demo where supported, the
image, document or other material. Its wiki page supplies a title, description,
relations and the account I want others to read. Reading the page, trying the
work and examining how it was made are connected views of that subject.

A proposed publish flow starts from the current work, opens a preview of what
others will receive, and lets me choose the page, material and related context
I want to share. It must show actual omissions and available audience choices.
A related private source or profile does not become public merely because it is
linked. Each published item keeps its connection to my world and revision.

The optional making-of view distinguishes the Skills/profile/harness I actually
used, where native evidence supplies that relation, from resources I recommend
or describe afterward. People can explore those relations without the interface
claiming that an advertised recipe is a recorded execution. Choosing to share
my whole profile remains an explicit operation over what that profile contains.

Opening a demo must preserve working assets and useful interaction. A source
textarea or inert screenshot cannot satisfy this journey. Executable content
gets the existing contained material surface and explicitly disclosed powers.
Unavailable formats receive honest owner-backed dispositions.

## Invite people into a NOW

Shared NOW is Central operating in the SpaceTimeDB shared installation with
Central's NOW protocol. Keep one consistent Central/wiki model. Users and agents
appear through nested Central projections/uploads of the portions of their worlds
and conditions they choose to expose; their participation also changes the field.
This is the owner's architectural direction; current support must be verified.

Every NOW is based on an intention plus one or more O:I worlds or world
projections. The intention states what this work is for; its world bindings name
the actual conditions and material brought into it. Selecting a node can seed
those bindings but does not substitute for the intention. Preserve their refs
and disclosed revisions through creation, participation and return.

A NOW is an ad hoc bubble of activity: this investigation, this task-context,
these participants, this duration and content of work. It anchors agent-time in
that actual situation. It can be public or private and can be ephemeral in the
shared space. Do not create a separately invented NOW-reading product or treat
it as an automatic replica of someone's private local NOW directory.

From a page, selected objects or a query constellation, I can propose a NOW,
name what we are doing, choose who can enter and what they may do, and invite
people or agents. The resulting bubble stays attached to those subjects. Entry,
contribution and agent action consume their existing owner operations.

The owner has accepted keeping selected outputs when a temporary NOW closes.
The closing experience lets participants identify the outputs to retain and their
permitted destinations; only the relevant owner operation makes that retention
durable. Keep the output connected to its originating intention, world bindings
and NOW without requiring the temporary working space to remain open. Selection
is not automatic wiki incorporation, source acceptance or broader publication.
If a retain/return operation fails, expose that failure and preserve its recovery
path before destructive closure.

The shared Central owner must specify lifecycle and retention for ephemeral
NOWs using its protocol: what closes, what is retained or returned, what is
withdrawn and what participants were allowed to keep. Do not silently reinterpret
existing snapshot-before-clean DAY semantics or promise that an expiring bubble
erases material another participant was authorised to retain. Exact close/return
UX follows that owner contract; it is an integration obligation, not a reason
to substitute a different experience.

Factory remains part of the desktop. A shared page can expose work originating
there, but creating a shared page or NOW does not require a Factory Run or turn
the shared experience into a Factory dashboard.

## One pane system; an optional accompanying agent

The right agent sidebar remains the person's access to their own accompanying
agent throughout the flow. Opening a shared node, file, participant profile or
NOW must not replace that agent sidebar with a shared-field inspector. The
agent's Conversation/Activity/Context/Inspect planes stay inside the agent
experience; field selection does not silently disclose content to that agent.

Shared Field uses the established canvas, panels, panes, tabs and popouts. It
does not add another permanent sidebar, navigation shell or window controller.
A full shared-field surface can occupy the canvas. Selecting a node focuses its
region: the relevant tree/constellation frames the subject on the left of that
surface, and its details and linked files can appear in a pane on the right.
That right-hand content pane belongs to the surface layout, not the separate
agent sidebar. Both use the existing resizable/collapsible pane grammar.

For example: I open somebody's demo node. Its nearby work remains visible on
the left; its description and linked files open beside it. I open a linked file
full-size or pop it into a native window while trying the demo. I can summon my
own agent alongside this exploration if I want help. Returning the popped-out
file restores it as a tab through the same docking actions used elsewhere.

The selected subject, its page, files and material retain canonical owner refs
through these moves. Full view, split view and popout are placements of surfaces,
not new objects, transcripts or copies of a draft. Returning from full view
restores the previous arrangement; re-docking restores the tab, selection and
caret/scroll/camera state. Define deterministic fallback placement if the old
pane no longer exists. Closing a native window and destroying semantic work
remain different actions under the existing window grammar.

## Foundation engineering — first execution rounds

First improve the production shell and make its existing surface system robust
enough to carry these journeys. Keep the three reference studies, corrected
native chrome, companion density and regional colours as the visual contract.
Do not mount every future feature to demonstrate foundation completeness.

Use one arrangement/action system across canvas panes, full views, popouts and
native menus. Each presentation receives owner refs/read models/actions,
availability/errors and bounded view state. Keep agent-session lifetime and
owner tasks independent of whether a particular view is mounted.

and interaction checks, not a full suite for each cosmetic adjustment.

The foundation receipt separates visual fidelity, functional/window correctness,
owner-contract compatibility and resource behaviour. No new native visual pass
has occurred in this specification turn. The execution operator still owes the

## Search forms constellations

The configurable Cmd-K/Ctrl-K aperture uses native VAK-backed resolution and
query operations across wiki and field. A person can bring notions, pages,
agents and people together to explore a constellation. Suggestions should make
the supported syntax learnable while retaining direct keyboard entry.

Do not invent operator spellings in the desktop. Completion, parsing, scope,
resolution and explanations use current owner contracts. A query preview may
reveal existing relations or a temporary combination; saving a relation, tagging
material, sharing a query or inviting an agent is a separate deliberate action.
The UI should make that distinction clear without requiring ontology vocabulary.

Proposed keyboard acceptance: arrow through query completions/results, Enter
opens the chosen result/constellation, Escape returns to the original caret;
back/forward restores the prior constellation and camera. The graph supports
meaningful node focus and opening, alongside trackpad/pointer navigation and
accessible list/tree equivalents. Resolve exact graph-navigation/pan bindings
against native shortcuts and writing/IME behaviour during implementation.

Selection, query and temporary constellation geometry are view state. They do
not silently add persistent semantic edges or disclose retrieved material to an
agent. Preserve workspace-scoped camera, focus and navigation history.

## Travel through the field

The owner's motion direction is spatial travel between actual graph
constellations: a short, smooth movement toward the next subject, with other
constellations providing depth and continuity. In dark mode, subdued distant
nodes can be shaded and gently glowing; arriving brings the relevant
constellation into readable focus. Light mode retains the paper/graphite family.

Use the existing point-cloud language for subtle aliveness, with slender
relations and deliberate density. Do not replace real graph neighbourhoods with
decorative stars or generate fictitious semantic neighbours. Display only
admitted relation data; bounded loading must remain efficient.

Travel is an interruptible navigation transition, not a forced loading sequence.
Keep a recognisable origin and destination, settle labels sharply, preserve
camera history, and handle rapid retargeting without queued flights. Data loading
and motion remain independent: missing data has an honest state, no cinematic
stall. Reduced-motion navigation keeps the same content/focus with restrained
transitions; animation does not encode the only indication of activity.

The existing shared loading/activity primitive is reused where applicable.
Subtle ambient field motion is a new owner direction beyond the current package's
static-at-rest rule. Ambient movement must be visually distinct from genuine
agent/task activity; it cannot imply a running provider. Review the resulting
package contract deliberately rather than silently contradicting it in CSS.
