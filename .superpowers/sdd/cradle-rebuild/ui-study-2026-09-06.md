  This is a subset of D19, not full split/session/depth restoration.

  successful-route familiarity remain later work.

## Limits and next work

layout uses authored sample positions. Agent responses, bounds and context are
illustrations. Navigation structure is sample content. Full wiki data loading,
route resolution, session hosting and projection transport remain business work.

Its suite is not green, including old rest/test-binding assumptions and real
workspace depth restoration failures. This study neither removes those checks
nor claims to resolve them. No design document was edited and no product pin
was changed.
