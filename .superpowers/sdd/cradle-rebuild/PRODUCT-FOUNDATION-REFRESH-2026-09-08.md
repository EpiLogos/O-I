## UX decisions submitted to the owner

**D-01 Default full-page emphasis — pending.** Recommended: conversation and
meaningful progress in the canvas, with selected Candidates/evidence/trajectory
opening as contextual work views. Alternative: Run overview first with conversation
alongside. The existing Factory GUI is an inspector, so simply embedding it would
leave two competing organising structures. In both cases agent details and the
Conversation/Activity/Context/Inspect chain belong in the dynamic right panel,
not a duplicated canvas section. Final plane-to-Factory mapping must be checked
against every interactive study, not inferred only from component names.

**D-02 Commission boundary — pending.** Recommended: conversation can precede a
Run; deliberate commissioning creates or binds its native Run and situated purpose.
Alternative: entering full-page work establishes a Run. An explicit boundary
preserves useful exploratory conversation and makes purpose/authority legible;
automatic creation reduces a transition but risks unwanted durable work records.
Neither presentation choice can implicitly grant execution or replace native IDs.
Do not implement either pending choice as an accepted product decision.

Detection disclosure, capability refusal, source provenance and preservation of
canonical identities above are contract requirements, not new UX votes. Placement
and depth should follow the accepted studies; bring any actual conflict back with
its observed reference and owner constraints rather than redesigning for convenience.
