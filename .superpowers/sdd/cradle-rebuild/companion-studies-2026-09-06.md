## Try these connected behaviours

1. Click O-I's files icon. Its chats are replaced by the file tree; the current
   conversation remains. Click the wiki icon: the project wiki becomes a tab.
2. Activate a chat tab. The sidebar reveals the project but remembers its lens.
   Browse Research files without replacing the canvas. Workspace selection is
   a separate action and restores that workspace's arrangement and sidebar modes.
3. In 03, write a draft in the chat and maximize it. Return with Escape. Use the
   tab context menu or drag to move it into the other group; the same draft stays.
4. Use Window/layout controls for columns, rows, studio or grid. Reducing groups
   consolidates their tabs rather than dropping sources. Divider arrow keys resize.
5. Open Central wiki, a project wiki and Shared Field from the sidebar. There are
   no in-graph scope/list controls. Private guidance stays out of the admitted
   Field reading. These are sample data, not live publication controls.
