## 4. Integration discipline

1. **No second semantic store.** Expressions carry refs/readings, not copied Wiki/Agent/Nara truth.
2. **No second renderer or engine clock.** Latest Point-Cloud native engine becomes the material body under O:I host.
3. **No stale adapter archaeology.** Consume current accepted owner contracts at execution time.
4. **No legacy debris as product architecture.** Migrate useful documents; retire obsolete runtime modes/presets once current representation covers them.
5. **Actions lead consequential work.** Selection/focus/presentation mechanics remain lightweight; domain/source/authority changes route through owners.
6. **Agent-native from the first contract.** Every meaningful application operation has structured state and result; no scraping as official API.
7. **Expression is not always full-screen.** Same capability supports focused instrument, ordinary embedded page and subtle/ambient presentation.
8. **Graph/Epii apertures are summonable.** Placement is presentation; subject/source/session identity is semantic.
9. **Human experience remains a gate.** Creative/joyful/alive are tested by the person after technical continuity/ownership is proven.
