> **This document is itself an action/artifact of clarifying the technology through the philosophy and the philosophy through the technology.** It develops the relation by which available source becomes an apprehensible Mark, the Mark becomes Aim, and metaphor, historical precedent, exemplary objects and other Figures can carry a genuine but bounded signal of the Whole.

The purpose is to give a human and an Agent a richer common account of **how intention becomes apprehensible before it becomes specification**.

---

## 0. Whole, Mark and the problem of apprehension

**Whole** is the structural term.

**The Mark** is the praxical term for that Whole insofar as an undertaking is answerable to it.

A Mark can be strongly present before it is exhaustively formalised. A human may know what a work is trying to become through a mixture of direct statement, taste, correction, image, example, analogy, remembered practice, desired experience, rejected form and encountered reality.

Artificial Agency therefore requires more than literal task parsing. It requires a disciplined relation between:

```text
available ground
      /
apprehension of the Whole
      /
directed undertaking
```

The Covenant names this more exactly through two nested `0/1` relations.

---

# I — Source / Mark

## 1. Source and Mark as a `0/1` relation

```text
0 — SOURCE-GROUND

    provenance-bearing material through which the intended Whole
    can become apprehensible:

    human expression
    authored position
    Project purpose and history
    accepted Decision
    direct observation
    implementation and returned reality
    existing Artifact
    image / diagram / metaphor
    historical precedent
    exemplary object or practice
    inherited symbolic material
    relevant cultural / technical convention

/

1 — MARK

    the articulated apprehension of the Whole to which
    the present undertaking is answerable
```

The relation is generative rather than classificatory.

Source-ground does not mechanically determine one Mark. The Agent receives a provenance-bearing field, follows its relations, and forms a situated reading of the Whole.

The Mark is therefore **determination from ground**, while remaining answerable to the ground from which it was apprehended.

This gives a recursive syntax:

```text
SOURCE
   0
   /
MARK
   1

MARK
   0
   /
AIM
   1
```

What becomes determination in one relation can become ground for the next.

```text
available ground
      ↓
MARK
    Whole becomes apprehensible
      ↓
AIM
    Whole becomes directive
      ↓
SCRIPT
    Whole becomes undertaken
      ↓
LIFE-WORLD
    undertaking meets actuality
      ↓
RETURN / RECOGNITION
    Whole becomes differently understood
```

The slash remains the important operation: continuity across the movement from available potential/ground into articulated determination.

---

## 2. Source is plural and provenance-bearing

`Source` here is a philosophical-praxical relation, not a new replacement software primitive.

The existing product field already carries concrete source identities through authored files, `Artifact`s, `Claim`s, `Evidence`, `Decision`s, `SourceIntegration`s, provider-native messages, repository state, observations and other native objects.

The Covenant asks what role those objects play in apprehending the Mark.

Consequential source material should retain its standing, for example:

```text
human-authored position
product / constitutional intent
design decision
architectural contract
implementation fact
direct observation
agent-generated interpretation or proposal
research proposition
experimental finding
community reproduction / field report
current development state
inference requiring verification
historical orienting precedent / Figure
```

These source standings answer different questions. Several can illuminate one Mark without becoming interchangeable forms of authority.

The Agent therefore preserves the relation:

```text
source object
    +
source standing / provenance
    +
relation to present question
        ↓
Mark-reading Claim
```

The Claim is the Agent's current articulation of the Mark. It does not retroactively become the human's authored source.

---

## 3. Marks and the Mark

The existing distinction remains:

```text
The Mark
    the Whole to which the undertaking is answerable

marks
    discrete symbolic or material articulations through which
    that Whole can be expressed, apprehended, interpreted,
    enacted, evidenced, remembered and returned
```

A source is encountered through marks.

A human sentence is a mark. A design document is a mark. A diagram is a mark. Code is a mark. An implementation observation is a mark. A historical technical object can be encountered through surviving marks. An Agent response can become a mark when retained.

No single mark necessarily exhausts the Mark.

This is why provenance and relation are essential: later Agency should be able to recover **what kind of mark this is, what it bears upon, and why it was retained**.

# II — Figure of the Mark

## 4. Definition

A **Figure of the Mark** is a symbolic, metaphorical, historical, exemplary, aesthetic, technical or experiential form through which some wholeness of an intended undertaking becomes apprehensible.

A Figure can include:

```text
metaphor
analogy
historical precedent
worked case
exemplary object
technical practice
image or diagram
story
ritual or social form
musical form
architectural form
natural phenomenon
remembered experience
```

A Figure matters because human intention is not exhausted by propositional specification.

A human can disclose enormous relational information through expressions such as:

```text
"this old instrument captures the move"
"imagine it working like this"
"this building / score / workshop / practice is the kind of whole I mean"
"the experience should have this relation"
```

The Figure is therefore a legitimate part of Source-ground.

It can let an Agent apprehend a gestalt before every constituent relation has been separately named. It can also let a human remain poietic: authoring toward a Whole through recognition, imagination and exemplary form rather than translating every intention into a pre-emptive machine specification.

---

## 5. Figure relation: `ILLUMINATES` and `BOUNDARY`

A Figure becomes technically and epistemically useful when two relations remain explicit:

```text
FIGURE
   │
   ├── ILLUMINATES ──► relation/aspect of the Mark
   │
   └── BOUNDARY ─────► what the Figure does not establish
```

### `ILLUMINATES`

What structural relation becomes easier to apprehend through this Figure?

Examples:

```text
an account of a world becoming an operable instrument
an authored whole becoming a score for distributed activity
a source corpus becoming a navigable atlas
a developmental process becoming a garden-like practice of cultivation
```

### `BOUNDARY`

What must not be inferred merely because the Figure is compelling?

A historical analogy can illuminate the **kind of move** being attempted without validating the project's ontology, formal laws, scientific claims or implementation choices.

A metaphor can orient a design without becoming runtime ontology.

An example can disclose experience without becoming a mandatory architecture.

The boundary relation keeps Figures generative rather than silently legislative.

---

## 6. Figure as source, Claim and Evidence

A Figure can have several epistemic relations at once:

```text
historical facts about the Figure
    can be supported by Evidence

Claim that the Figure illuminates relation R
    is an interpretive / design Claim

Claim that relation R is therefore true of the Project
    requires independent Project ground
```

For example:

```text
historical source
    establishes what an instrument/practice actually did

Agent or human interpretation
    Claim: this historical form illuminates our intended relation X

Project source / design / implementation
    separately establishes whether X is actually part of this Project
```

This three-part discipline prevents analogy from being laundered into evidence for a stronger proposition.

# III — Human poiesis and artificial apprehension

## 7. The human can author through Figures

The Covenant gives human authorship more expressive range than requirements capture.

A human may author through:

```text
ordinary prose
technical specification
philosophical distinction
correction
refusal
example
counterexample
historical case
image
metaphor
analogy
diagram
music
prototype
felt or desired experience
```

The Agent's task is to follow the relation that gives the material significance in this World.

This is a practical consequence of treating the human as the leading authorial perspective of the Covenant. The human can remain concise, poetic, partial or exploratory while the artificial side undertakes deeper recovery of source, Project relation, current actuality and possible consequence.

The purpose is not to romanticise ambiguity. It is to make **meaningful forms of human authorship operable without forcing premature formalisation**.

---

## 8. Agent reception of a Figure

When a Figure appears to be important, the Agent should be able to perform this compact movement:

```text
RECEIVE
What Figure has actually been offered or recovered?

GROUND
What is known about the Figure itself, and from what source?

ILLUMINATE
What relation of the present Mark does it make more apprehensible?

BOUND
Where does the analogy stop?

RELATE
Which authored Project positions, current Claims, Decisions or observations
independently support the relation being illuminated?

USE
What does the Figure change about understanding, design imagination,
explanation, or the present undertaking?

RETURN
Express the useful relation without silently promoting the Figure into canon.
```

The Figure can remain available as an orienting mark for later Agency, with its provenance and interpretive standing intact.

# IV — Figure through the Situation Script

## 9. Becoming / Unbecoming refraction

A Figure can participate directly in [[COVENANT-SITUATION-SCRIPT]] without becoming a fifth lens or new ontology.

### `L2` — Determination

Ask:

```text
What does the Figure actually support me in claiming?
What Claim about the Mark am I forming through it?
What is established about the Figure itself?
What remains analogy, interpretation or open possibility?
```

`ILLUMINATES` and `BOUNDARY` are principally disciplined here.

### `L3` — Undertaking

Ask:

```text
What possible form of undertaking becomes visible through this Figure?
What relation of actors, operations, practices or transformations does it suggest?
What does it let human and Agent imagine together?
```

The Figure can participate in ingression and possible form without automatically determining the selected design.

### `L2′` — Transformation

Ask:

```text
What actual material or technical form does the Figure help make thinkable?
Which part of that form can be enacted in the present life-world?
What must be adapted because our material conditions differ?
```

### `L3′` — History

Ask:

```text
What lineage does the Figure place the undertaking within?
What does the historical development of the Figure reveal about revision,
practice, error, adaptation, institutional context or eventual supersession?
What can the Project learn from that trajectory without imitating it?
```

The Situation Script therefore lets a Figure move from inspiration into disciplined praxis while preserving its source standing.

# V — Relation to response matrices and development instruments

## 10. Generic response-matrix relation

A response/capability matrix used by Software Factory or another agentic Project is a **generalisable development instrument**. Its axes, participants and contents are Project-native. It can be carried out by one Agent, several Agents, a human–Agent collective or another suitable process.

Nothing in the generic matrix protocol requires:

```text
Epi-Logos
M0–M5
six Guardians
MEF
QL coordinates
```

Those structures can become the **content or refraction of a matrix for an Epi Project**. They do not define the generic protocol.

The Covenant contributes at the generic level by supplying:

```text
Source-ground
Mark
Aim
Claim / Evidence standing
Context Frame / scale
undertaking
Return
Recognition
```

A matrix can therefore be understood as one possible structured instrument through which a Project makes its present relational/developmental field inspectable while retaining bearing toward its Mark.

### Historical table/canon forms as secondary design inspiration

Historical computational practices can sometimes illuminate useful matrix design qualities:

```text
structured inspectable state
procedures / canons for operating it
worked derivation or intermediate state
version / revision history
situated application
```

This is a transferable design lesson, not an assertion that response matrices are intrinsically astronomical tables or that any particular Project ontology belongs in Factory Core.

# VI — Worked external instantiation: Epi-Logos and the Alfonsine Figure

## 11. Why the example is external to O:I canon

Epi-Logos provides a rich current Project in which this general Figure relation can be seen concretely.

Its specific M/M′, Guardian, Bimba/Pratibimba, archetypal-correspondential and bioquaternionic semantics remain **Epi-owned domain content**. They are not universal O:I or Factory ontology.

Within that Project, the Alfonsine Tables / wider zīj tradition can function as an orienting historical Figure for the attempt to render an account of world-relations into an operable situated computational instrument.

The Epi-specific treatment belongs in the QL-MEF/Epi integration source, not here.

The O:I-level lesson is the Figure relation itself:

```text
historical / exemplary source
        ↓
Figure of the Mark
        ↓
ILLUMINATES + BOUNDARY
        ↓
Mark-reading Claim
        ↓
Aim / Script / design imagination
        ↓
actual Project evidence and Return remain authoritative
```

# VII — Operational representation

## 12. Represent the relation before minting primitives

No new canonical `Figure`, `Mark` or `Source` runtime primitive is required merely because the conceptual relation is useful.

A current implementation can represent the field through existing objects:

```text
Mark-reading Claim
    subject / Context Frame Ref
    source_refs
    Evidence refs
    larger-Whole refs
    Decision refs

Orienting Figure
    source/artifact Ref
    descriptive Claim Ref
    ILLUMINATES Claim/relation
    BOUNDARY Claim/relation
    Project/Run/Situation relevance
```

This can be tested through Skills/Methods, Context composition and evaluation before any schema promotion.

A first-class contract would earn its place if returned implementation experience shows stable identity is required for cross-Run retrieval, multi-Agent coordination, versioning, comparison or other concrete operations.

---

## 13. Operational parity questions

The conceptual relation becomes useful technology only if it changes practice.

Ask:

1. Can an Agent recover the intended Whole more reliably when provenance-bearing Source-ground is available?
2. Can it distinguish a Mark-reading Claim from the human-authored material which informed it?
3. Can a Figure improve comprehension of a difficult Project relation without being promoted into false ontology?
4. Can `ILLUMINATES` and `BOUNDARY` remain visible enough that later Agents inherit the useful analogy and its limit together?
5. Can humans use metaphor, historical precedent, images and exemplary forms naturally without having to translate them into an engineering schema first?
6. Does the Situation Script turn Figures into better judgement and undertaking rather than ornamental prose?
7. Can returned implementation and observation revise the Mark-reading even when the orienting Figure remains useful?
8. Can generic Factory/agentic protocols consume Mark/Aim discipline without inheriting Epi-specific ontology?

# VIII — Compact statement

```text
Source is the available provenance-bearing ground.

The Mark is the Whole apprehended from that ground insofar as an undertaking
is answerable to it.

A Figure of the Mark is a metaphor, historical precedent, exemplary object,
image, practice or other form through which some relation of that Whole becomes
more apprehensible.

A Figure carries two explicit relations: what it ILLUMINATES and the BOUNDARY
beyond which it does not establish the Project's truth.

The human can author poietically through Figures as well as through specification.
The Agent can receive those Figures as meaningful source, form provenance-bearing
Claims about what they disclose, and use them within the Situation Script.

Source / Mark and Mark / Aim form nested 0/1 relations:

    SOURCE / MARK / AIM

available ground becomes apprehensible Whole; apprehensible Whole becomes
directed undertaking.

Actual Project source, evidence, implementation and returned reality remain
answerable for what is really true and what has actually been made.
```
