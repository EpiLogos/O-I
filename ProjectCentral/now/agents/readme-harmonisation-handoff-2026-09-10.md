## The canonical language (lock this verbatim where it matters)

**The decisive distinction:** Objective Internality ≠ Subjective Immediacy.

- **Objective Internality** — the structured field of objects, events and relations that constitutes the world of a being.
- **Subjective Immediacy** — that world's first-person appearing.
- No absolute external observer is smuggled back in. What appears external is disclosed from within an internality; relations between beings are relations among internalities within larger fields of co-internality.

**World and Life** is the first language:

- **A World** — the whole field within which something can appear, persist, relate and matter.
- **A Life** — a self-disclosure within an internality which, in disclosing itself, also discloses the World in which it is contained.

**Logos / Vāk** is articulation in this deeper register — not human speech, but difference becoming determinate, relations becoming expressed, a field disclosing itself through events. Human language is one developed form; thought, perception, gesture, action, machine tokens, tool calls and agent acts are further determinations of the same problem.
