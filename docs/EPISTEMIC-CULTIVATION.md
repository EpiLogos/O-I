## The cultural shift

A mature AI field does not only engineer models and applications. It can also treat **the crafting of epistemologies as a first-class role**.

That means deliberately shaping the forms of disclosure, distinction, relation, evidence, uncertainty, contradiction, synthesis and return through which artificial agency learns and acts.

The work includes human and agent activity such as:

- source and corpus selection;
- structural and relational annotation;
- QL/MEF-aligned decomposition and recomposition;
- generation of dialogical training/evaluation material;
- human editorial return over agent-generated material;
- contrastive and counterfactual dataset construction;
- model adaptation and intervention;
- evaluation of both epistemic behaviour and model-interior organisation.

This is not merely data labelling around a fixed model. The epistemic field itself is an authored and revisable object.

## Figures, source-ground and cultivated apprehension

The Covenant adds an important relation to this work: an epistemic environment does not shape judgement only by supplying propositions. It can also make a **Whole / Mark apprehensible** through well-grounded Figures — metaphors, historical precedents, exemplary objects, diagrams, worked cases, practices and other forms which disclose a relation compactly.

The relation is:

```text
SOURCE-GROUND
      0
      /
MARK
      1
```

A Figure is one possible provenance-bearing mark inside Source-ground. Its use remains disciplined through two explicit relations:

```text
FIGURE
   ├── ILLUMINATES ──► what relation / aspect becomes more apprehensible
   └── BOUNDARY ─────► what the Figure does not establish
```

This matters for epistemic cultivation because examples and analogies can strongly shape what an Agent notices, expects and regards as a possible form of action. They can therefore be deliberately authored and evaluated rather than treated as incidental rhetoric.

A cultivated Figure should preserve at least:

```text
source / provenance
what is factually established about the Figure
what present relation it is intended to illuminate
what stronger Claim it does not establish
how its use changed interpretation / judgement / action
```

The distinction between Figure and proof is part of the epistemic craft. Historical Evidence about an example can establish the example's actual form; it cannot automatically validate an analogous Project proposition.

This creates useful experimental questions for Actuation research:

- does a well-chosen Figure improve an Agent's recovery of a difficult Project Mark?
- does carrying `ILLUMINATES` and `BOUNDARY` together reduce analogy-driven hallucination or false ontology promotion?
- can multiple Figures disclose complementary relations without collapsing into one imposed framing?
- how do Figures interact with Claims, Evidence, retrieval and later retained Context?
- can human-authored metaphors and examples remain poietic while artificial Agency makes their operative relation inspectable?

Figures can therefore become first-class **epistemic artifacts** without becoming first-class universal runtime primitives.

## Objective Internality

Epistemic cultivation extends the Objective Internality programme in a precise way.

An agent's operative internal world is not shaped only by spontaneous model organisation. It can also be conditioned by deliberately authored external structures that return into later acts:

```text
sources
corpora
annotations
dialogues
lens structures
orienting Figures
model adapters
experiment histories
findings
critiques
```

These structures can become objectively inspectable parts of the conditions under which later artificial judgement occurs.

The relevant circuit is:

```text
human / agent epistemic craft
        ↓
objective artifact
        ↓
model / agent cultivation
        ↓
changed judgement and action
        ↓
returned observation / finding
        ↺
```

The fact that some epistemic organisation is deliberately cultivated matters. Human authorship, agent generation, source derivation, transformation and training lineage should remain visible rather than being redescribed as if the model self-organised the whole field alone.

## Objective Co-Internality

Epistemologies can also become shared research objects.

A local system may selectively project:

- a corpus or corpus revision;
- an annotation set;
- an orienting Figure with its source, `ILLUMINATES` and `BOUNDARY` relations;
- a QL/MEF disclosure protocol;
- a training/adaptation recipe;
- a model/checkpoint/adapter reference;
- an experiment;
- an evaluation;
- a model-interior observation or visualisation;
- a structural finding;
- a reproduction, critique, correction or alternative interpretation.

Other humans and Agents can Encounter those objects and return attributable Contributions.

```text
Actuation research object
        ↓ Projection
SharedField
        ↓ Encounter
Human / Agent Participant
        ↓ Contribution
critique / reproduction / extension / correction
        ↓
SharedField
        ↺
```

This makes epistemic cultivation social without creating a single hosted epistemic authority.

Projection does not transfer canonical ownership. A ranking or evaluation over an epistemic artifact remains an attributable Contribution, not an invisible truth layer.

## QL / MEF relation

The base sixfold can organise the wider {O:I} architecture and research process as a design primitive without requiring every object to carry an explicit QL label.

For explicit model research, however, the full QL/MEF system matters:

- QL as positional, relational, processual and topological grammar;
- conjugacy, recursion, inversion and return;
- MEF as full-field refraction rather than topic classification;
- L0/L0′ as a minimum disclosure floor;
- QL/MEF-aligned corpus and dialogue construction;
- model-interior and agent-level comparison of different disclosure trajectories.

Actuation is where those structures are made experimentally operative. {O:I} is where the resulting work can appear in the shared objective-internal field.

## Research commons

The future O:I Wiki / shared research surface should therefore be capable of representing epistemic work as first-class material rather than only papers and model benchmarks.

A useful research commons can make visible:

```text
source → epistemic transformation → corpus revision
       → Figure / disclosure protocol
       → model/intervention → experiment
       → observation → finding
       → critique/reproduction → revised epistemic artifact
```

The important cultural move is that **epistemologies themselves become things we can author, compare, contest, cultivate and return**.

That is the O:I relation to the Actuation programme: not ownership of the laboratory, but a shared field in which the laboratory's epistemic artifacts can acquire durable social life.
