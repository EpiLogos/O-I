# O:I product-fidelity review — 19 September 2026

**Desk:** O:I / whole composition. **Finding prefix:** OI. **Standing:** source-evidence review, with explicitly incomplete coverage; not a suite acceptance, installed-cut receipt, replacement Wayfinder or permission to change production.

This report preserves the commissioned whole while distinguishing what was actually traced from what remains unverified. It establishes five actionable source findings/risk cases and records useful existing implementation. It does **not** certify the complete capability field: in particular, the complete Material NOW operation chain, Skill-edit-to-effective-use chain, external-harness first-task chain and destination-UI source-to-Expression return have not been demonstrated. Those are substantive uncovered obligations, not implied successes or inferred defects.

## 1. Intended product experience, authority and scope

The governing sources are the [review protocol](https://github.com/EpiLogos/O-I/blob/a5012f5aa44d0fed317a261b009250d70013ccb5/docs/reviews/product-fidelity-2026-09-19/README.md), [baseline](https://github.com/EpiLogos/O-I/blob/a5012f5aa44d0fed317a261b009250d70013ccb5/docs/reviews/product-fidelity-2026-09-19/BASELINE.json), [Founding Positions](https://github.com/EpiLogos/O-I/blob/a1c7010fa290219d58f78ee03f55651ac14ed75d/docs/positions/FOUNDING-POSITIONS.md), the [experience spine](https://github.com/EpiLogos/O-I/blob/a1c7010fa290219d58f78ee03f55651ac14ed75d/docs/experience/README.md), [Harness-first Adoption](https://github.com/EpiLogos/O-I/blob/a1c7010fa290219d58f78ee03f55651ac14ed75d/docs/experience/HARNESS-FIRST-ADOPTION.md), [Configuration Plane](https://github.com/EpiLogos/O-I/blob/a1c7010fa290219d58f78ee03f55651ac14ed75d/docs/cradle/09-CONFIGURATION-PLANE.md), [Workcell NOW temporal field](https://github.com/EpiLogos/O-I/blob/a1c7010fa290219d58f78ee03f55651ac14ed75d/docs/experience/WORKCELL-NOW-TEMPORAL-FIELD.md), and the actual amendments to [#375](https://github.com/EpiLogos/O-I/issues/375). Reading coverage is qualified in §3; listing a source here is not a claim that every linked obligation was exhausted.

O:I is the relation in which the native products become usable together, not a new owner of all their state. Central supplies durable ground/source/Project relations; Actuation supplies agency and authority; AIKit supplies powers, practice and harness/session relations; Factory supplies developmental work and its evidence/recognition; Workcell supplies the material body. QL is a native participant whose internals are excluded here. The six review desks do not redefine the six native products.

The positive requirement is stronger than passing payloads: a person or an existing harness must be able to find the actual capability, understand its subject and prerequisites, exercise it with the proper authority, and encounter its result or refusal in the chosen working surface. World, Project, source, subject, presentation, projected edition and live encounter must remain related without becoming interchangeable. A source reference is neither a copied body nor permission to act. Available context is not automatically selected or effectively loaded context.

The complementary negative architecture is equally specific: no silent replacement of owner-native truth with an O:I mirror; no claim that installed means running or projected means loaded; no mode change that secretly installs, launches or grants; no current-looking display of an unrelated or held result; no renderer geometry promoted into semantic identity; no alternate Guardian or Skill ownership invented to fill a UI gap.

## 4. Positive architecture and existing implementation to retain

### Native discovery and routing are substantive, not merely labels

At the baseline, [`catalog_source.rs`](https://github.com/EpiLogos/O-I/blob/a1c7010fa290219d58f78ee03f55651ac14ed75d/cli/src/catalog_source.rs) distinguishes override, adopted runtime catalogue and embedded source. An unreadable runtime catalogue is not silently replaced by a convenient embedded answer. [`product_command_route.rs`](https://github.com/EpiLogos/O-I/blob/a1c7010fa290219d58f78ee03f55651ac14ed75d/cli/src/product_command_route.rs) preserves native arguments and explicit executable selection; invalid active-receipt errors are not simply discarded to fall back to stale PATH. These distinctions should remain visible through Inspect in the receiving UI.

The final [registry/routing diff](https://github.com/EpiLogos/O-I/commit/a5012f5aa44d0fed317a261b009250d70013ccb5) repairs a real split: a developer composition registration could dispatch one binary while configuration discovery consulted a different catalogue/PATH binary. Preserve the registered executable resolution and captured stderr. The final diff's test claims are useful provenance, not this review's execution evidence. A remaining all-selection-modes test should compare dispatch and configuration discovery for overrides, active suite and registration rather than assuming one repaired path covers all of them.

### Composition state is lawful and necessary

The [Configuration Plane](https://github.com/EpiLogos/O-I/blob/a1c7010fa290219d58f78ee03f55651ac14ed75d/docs/cradle/09-CONFIGURATION-PLANE.md) permits O:I-owned sparse desired profiles, overlays, provenance and reconciliation. It does not permit O:I to redefine a native setting or claim a desired value is effective. [`liveSource.ts`](https://github.com/EpiLogos/O-I/blob/7747d5e67d2814a29a44788d0b901750cb553215/desktop/cradle/src/configuration/liveSource.ts), `applyProfileUse` and `saveProfile`, explicitly keep profile-use/edit separate from native apply. The native [`OwnerTransport`](https://github.com/EpiLogos/O-I/blob/a1c7010fa290219d58f78ee03f55651ac14ed75d/cli/src/configuration/kernel/transport.rs) carries opaque owner plans. Those are good boundaries; OI-003 is a loss in the consumer round trip, not a reason to remove the plane.

Workcell's [`config_apply`](https://github.com/EpiLogos/Workcell/blob/04160f51d37e78d600f32440b4f5275c02b78e9c/crates/workcell-cli/src/main.rs) verifies the owner-minted plan digest before writing, validates the declared-services value, and recognizes replay by changeset/setting/scope/digest. It returns a native history reference and a no-op receipt for a known replay. Preserve that native truth. A declaration update is not a claim that a process has been started.

### Presentation state is not owner duplication

The inspected [`workspace/store.ts`](https://github.com/EpiLogos/O-I/blob/7747d5e67d2814a29a44788d0b901750cb553215/desktop/cradle/src/workspace/store.ts) keeps workspace navigation and view preferences, uses Project identity, preserves recoverable writing, and handles corrupt persistence rather than treating an unreadable record as consent to overwrite it. The corresponding [`KernelProvider`](https://github.com/EpiLogos/O-I/blob/7747d5e67d2814a29a44788d0b901750cb553215/desktop/cradle/src/kernel/KernelProvider.tsx) bounds recent renderer receipts to 256, serializes explicit operations, associates errors with the addressed source/operation, and preserves working drafts on the relevant native outcomes. These are foundations to reuse, not a second native source store to delete.

The current Factory handoff makes Desk sources, selected Run, centre view and held return position **display preferences**. That is legitimate composition. It must not become a parallel Run database or an inferred “current Run” assigned to a Direct conversation.

### Identity and bounded embodiment already have concrete support

The inspected [`expression/world.ts`](https://github.com/EpiLogos/O-I/blob/7747d5e67d2814a29a44788d0b901750cb553215/desktop/cradle/src/expression/world.ts) and [`expression_world.rs`](https://github.com/EpiLogos/O-I/blob/7747d5e67d2814a29a44788d0b901750cb553215/desktop/cradle/kernel/src/expression_world.rs) preserve native references, explicit revisions, bounded local wholes and selection distinct from invocation. Geometry is a presentation parameter, not authority to manufacture a relation. The native [Central source horizon](https://github.com/EpiLogos/Central/blob/0a58a32c7587f5576ace4239a1fd6d9bde868510/ctrl/src/source_horizon.rs) distinguishes binding/provenance/revision/change data and retrieval permission. These are useful producer/consumer building blocks; they do not alone prove the complete Library/page/Expression journey.

The [`material.rs` consumer](https://github.com/EpiLogos/O-I/blob/7747d5e67d2814a29a44788d0b901750cb553215/desktop/cradle/kernel/src/material.rs) explicitly distinguishes sequential observation from an atomic snapshot, checks the expected World, and carries uncertain-operation failure information. Preserve that caution: loss of a subprocess response must not invite blind replay of a potentially executed action.

Actuation's [dispatch table](https://github.com/EpiLogos/Actuation/blob/47f4fa3c184850e254ef5089696bf5fbbad907ec/crates/actuation-cli/src/dispatch.rs) is shared by command discovery/help/dispatch. ## 6. Findings

### OI-001 — Managed update changes the dispatch link before verification and receipt publication

**Seam:** O:I `update_flow::apply_entry` → `stage_and_link` / `point_activation` → managed receipt and executable consumers. **Activity/authority:** explicitly apply a managed update while preserving the old working installation on failure; Harness-first Adoption and the updater's own declared failure contract.

**Positive requirement / retained implementation.** Build an exact committed source cut without mutating an occupied checkout, keep content-addressed artifacts, report the selected source separately from running processes, and publish a truthful installed relation. Preserve the archive-based build and bounded reusable build cache.

**Negative requirement.** A failed verification must not leave the default dispatch link pointing at the failed candidate while the durable installation account still describes the previous one.

**Paired evidence.** Final [`update_flow.rs`](https://github.com/EpiLogos/O-I/blob/a5012f5aa44d0fed317a261b009250d70013ccb5/cli/src/update_flow.rs), `stage_and_link`, performs `atomic_symlink(&bin_link, &relative)` before returning. `apply_entry` calls that function **before** rehashing the staged file and running its version smoke check. It can then return an error. An existing activation link already addressing the managed `bin/<exe>` observes that change. The function comment nevertheless says “On failure nothing is linked; the store and receipts keep their previous contents.” The comment is contradicted by the order of effects.

**Standing / revisions.** Static-proven failure-order defect for the final O:I source, also observed in the baseline. No physical failure injected. The broader multi-product receipt/rollback behavior was not exhaustively proven; the single-entry ordering is sufficient for this finding. No installed-cut conclusion.

**Consequence / priority / confidence.** High priority for the updater line: a failed update can change what the next invocation runs. High confidence in the static ordering; occurrence and machine impact are unmeasured. This report does not authorize changing the concurrent Mac cut.

**Smallest restoration.** Stage and validate an immutable candidate without altering public links; only after validation publish the intended activation and its corresponding durable record. Preserve explicitly partial multi-owner semantics rather than claiming an unimplemented global transaction. Define recovery for any publication interruption and retain the previous artifact. Correct the misleading comment after behavior is established. No new installer or owner mirror.

**Receiving experience.** Existing update status/Inspect should distinguish built, verified, activated and running. On failure, show which activation remains and a bounded retry/recovery action; do not say “unchanged” unless the links and record agree. These are proposed copy distinctions, not a new screen.

**Discriminating proof / placement.** Later repository test through the original `oi update --apply` entry: begin with a verified working activation; provide a staged candidate whose version check fails; assert nonzero result, unchanged resolved dispatch target and coherent receipt. Then use a valid candidate and assert publication/readback. Add an interruption case around publication and reuse existing managed-update fixtures. Pure repository/sandbox proof first; installation/running readback belongs to the native cut crew, not this report.

### OI-002 — “Current” validates executable presence, not activation identity

**Seam:** O:I `update_flow::plan_product` → `print_plan_report` → human/agent currency judgement. **Activity/authority:** ask whether the installed selection is current, rather than merely whether an old artifact remains on disk.

**Positive requirement.** Preserve the efficient receipt/hash check and avoid gratuitous rebuilding. The disclosed activation must resolve to the artifact whose identity is being reported.

**Negative requirement and evidence.** In final [`plan_product`](https://github.com/EpiLogos/O-I/blob/a5012f5aa44d0fed317a261b009250d70013ccb5/cli/src/update_flow.rs), `healthy` checks the recorded managed artifact's digest and calls `is_executable` on `entry.bin` and `entry.activation`. It does not establish that those paths resolve to that recorded artifact. `print_plan_report` renders `PlanAction::Skip` as `current`. A retained valid artifact plus a retargeted but executable activation satisfies these checks.

**Standing / revisions.** Static-proven insufficient predicate at baseline/final O:I; safe retargeted-link reproduction not executed. No assertion that any user's links are currently wrong. Independent of OI-001: it can misclassify drift even without a failed update.

**Consequence / priority / confidence.** High diagnostic priority; high confidence in the predicate gap. It can obscure source/install divergence and defeat a person's attempt to repair a stale runtime selection. It does not itself prove a security exploit or actual wrong resident process.

**Restoration and compatibility.** Compare the effective activation chain with the expected immutable artifact using a deliberate cross-platform policy; disclose ambiguous/unmanaged paths rather than inferring equality from executability. Preserve valid symlink/launcher arrangements where supported, and do not conflate installed activation with an already-running inode. Prefer a repair action over an unnecessary rebuild when only activation is wrong.

**Receiving experience.** Existing update/System/Inspect surfaces should show desired cut, verified stored artifact, effective activation target and separately observed running version. Proposed wording: “artifact verified; activation differs” rather than “current.”

**Proof / placement.** Through `oi update` planning, start with a valid receipt and artifact, then direct activation to a second harmless executable. Current code selects Skip; corrected code must disclose drift without modifying anything. Restore the link and prove Skip. Exercise supported platform link behavior in native CI/sandbox; physical running-process distinction belongs to the returned cut. Reuse updater tests rather than invent another health service.

### OI-003 — App Apply loses the exact owner plan the person reviewed

**Seam:** O:I `createLiveConfigPlaneSource.apply` → `configuration::Client::apply` → owner `config apply --plan-file`; concrete owner: Workcell `config_apply`. **Activity/authority:** inspect an owner-native plan, approve that plan, and receive its exact result. Authority: Configuration Plane §§6–9 and the source's plan/digest contract.

**Positive requirement / retained code.** Keep generic presentation, sparse desired state, native validation, opaque native plan/digest, owner idempotence and post-read reconciliation. Workcell already checks its minted digest and returns owner-native history receipts. O:I's native transport can carry the raw plan.

**Lost relation / paired evidence.** In pushed [`liveSource.ts`](https://github.com/EpiLogos/O-I/blob/7747d5e67d2814a29a44788d0b901750cb553215/desktop/cradle/src/configuration/liveSource.ts), `plan` stores a `plan_digest → ChangeRequest` mapping. `apply(plans)` looks up the requests and sends `{op: "config_apply", requests: ...}`; the reviewed plan body/digest is not in the outgoing operation. The receiving [`configuration::Client::apply`](https://github.com/EpiLogos/O-I/blob/7747d5e67d2814a29a44788d0b901750cb553215/desktop/cradle/kernel/src/configuration.rs) constructs a new requested changeset and invokes `oi config apply --request-file - --json`. This is not transport of the previously displayed owner plan. Close view remains distinct from stopping native work. Useful recovery belongs in the visible pane, not only logs.
