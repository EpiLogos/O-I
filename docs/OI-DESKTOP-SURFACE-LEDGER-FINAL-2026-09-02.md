- **private/unprojected state:** local/private source does not become hosted merely through addressability or renderer reuse;
- **hosted/provider failure:** existing SpaceTimeDB subscription tests retain the last good local Explore application rather than replacing it with authoritative emptiness;
- **provider degradation:** Session Observatory emits truthful degraded Activity; Herdr/provider-loss tests preserve unrelated SessionSpace/AgentSession/provider relations;
- **version/basis conflict:** Central/VersionedWorld V7 refuses blind stale-basis application; Factory P5 refuses changed AIKit P4 basis;
- **semantic identity under provider material:** AIKit VersionedWorld rejects ProjectRef rebinding; Herdr/worktree/Hyprland/provider identities remain material/presentation evidence;
- **renderer security:** current desktop remains behind the Rust/Tauri allowlisted bridge; rich/shared renderers do not gain ambient shell/fs/process/network/secret authority;
- **credential presentation:** current AIKit credential convergence keeps raw secret material out of ordinary composition state;
- **Workcell/provider absence:** material/provider operation remains availability evidence and is not fabricated by O:I;
- **QL/Epi degraded provider:** predecessor §11 explicitly preserves fixture/derived-only truth and refuses a false live-now claim.

Physical/provider facts not exercised remotely remain exactly that: **unproven physical facts**, not deterministic application gaps.
