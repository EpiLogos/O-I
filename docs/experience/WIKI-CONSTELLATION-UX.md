# Wiki and constellation practice in the existing UX spine

**Standing:** owner-directed intended experience and development clarification, 20 September 2026. Publication is requested; runtime and human-use evidence remain separate. This extends the existing KN, WR, AG, PX, UI and SH stories, the Expression field and QL UX spine. It creates no new story family or capability registry and does not ratify unrelated H-pending sources.

**Why and decisions:** [WC01–WC15](../positions/WIKI-CONSTELLATION-PRACTICE.md). **How to build:** [joined Wayfinder](../../.wayfinder/maps/wiki-constellation-development.md). **Detailed contracts:** [specification](../cradle/WIKI-CONSTELLATION-SPEC.md). **Machine-readable attachment to existing stories:** [wiki-constellation.json](wiki-constellation.json).

## 1. What the person should experience

I bring my existing project notes or writing into the system. They are immediately useful as linked documents: I can read properly rendered Markdown, follow a wikilink, see where a passage is cited, browse tags, search and return without waiting for the whole file tree again. I do not have to classify my writing through QL.

When I want to work more deeply, I gather particular passages, images or linked subjects and begin a constellation. I can start from the material or choose a QL frame and explore what belongs in its positions. I can leave a position open, change my interpretation or compare another organisation. The same source can have a different role in another constellation.

In Technè this is real making: I select glyphs, arrange objects in three dimensions, connect them with lines or particle relations, write alongside them, bring in images and compose Scenes. Epii can work with me on the same construction. When I move a member or reject a proposed relation, the agent encounters that revised work, not a stale description of it in chat.

I can save the constellation, develop an Expression or Journey, and compose these into a Palace. The new writing and deliberate QL relationships return to the Wiki. Later I can find them, understand where they came from, reuse them or revise them. The original sources are still there, and changing a source identifies the affected interpretations without destroying all my composition.

## 2. Decisions visible in the encounter

**Ordinary Wiki is complete without QL.** Source linking is not a preparation exercise for the "real" product. Good typography, backlinks, tags, previews, keyboard navigation, filtering and fast return are first-class work.

**Constellation-making is an authoring act.** A person need not wait for a pre-certified structure. A chosen QL form can be an explicit provisional interpretation; its formal validity does not certify its claims.

**Technè is an expressive construction medium.** The full existing 3D Expression system is the body of the work, not a demo thumbnail or a flat export.

**The work is cumulative.** Saved constellations, typed QL relationships and generated artifacts return through native operations to the connected source field. A visual connection is not silently a semantic assertion, but deliberate relational authoring must actually be possible.

**Continuity is part of the product.** Source reads and working models live above disposable views in the existing kernel resource layer. Switching tabs, modes or lenses does not recreate the work. These decisions are expanded with their reasons in WC01–WC15, not hidden in a schema.

## 3. Whole activities and failure branches

### A. Read and traverse an existing linked collection

**Existing stories:** KN01, KN02, KN03, KN09; UI01, UI02, UI05. **Decisions:** WC01, WC02, WC13.

The person opens a real document, follows an internal link, previews its target, reads a backlink excerpt and returns to the same paragraph. They search by remembered words or alias without knowing the filename. The graph can show the same link occurrence and its direction, and tags offer useful collection routes without pretending to assert relations between all tagged documents.

The agent resolves the same sources through native knowledge operations and can explain which passage supports an answer. It distinguishes source occurrences, authored knowledge and suggestions; it does not invent a semantic edge from a matching label.

Ambiguous titles show distinct candidates and locations. Missing targets remain unresolved, not blank successful pages. A slow supplementary provider does not withhold useful local results. Reader, graph and agent lookup must agree on the exact reference and revision.

### B. Filter a field without changing what it means

**Existing stories:** KN02, KN03; UI01, UI05. **Decisions:** WC05, WC11, WC13.

The person restricts scope, kind, relation family, direction or depth, or searches within the graph. Applied filters remain visible. Clear filters and Fit do different things. The quiet small-node/thin-line view stays readable; emphasis groups and label density are optional presentation tools.

Selecting two members of a larger constellation retains or explicitly hides its permitted structural context. It does not manufacture a smaller QL form. The person can distinguish matched, contextual and displayed material, and can restore a saved view with camera and reading position intact.

The agent receives the admitted scope/query/selection rather than a screenshot. Filtered-away neighbours, truly absent links, unavailable providers and private material are not conflated. Keyboard and accessible-list routes must perform the same operations as pointer gestures.

### C. Make a constellation from material or from a frame

**Existing stories:** KN02, KN06, KN08, KN07; WR03; QL UX04, UX05, UX06. **Decisions:** WC03–WC07.

The person selects exact passages or related subjects and chooses Add to constellation, or starts with a QL frame and question. They place members, assign contextual roles, annotate, connect/reconnect relations, compare an alternative and leave real gaps visible. They can create new writing deliberately rather than the system fabricating material to fill a position.

M0′ gathers the available corpus and initiates work; M1′ makes deeper construction tools available in the same field. A member can appear in two constellations with different roles. Reusing a constellation, editing its native definition, changing an instance's presentation and making a variant are recognisably different acts.

Formal validation and evidential standing are separately inspectable. Exploration is allowed. A sixfold drawing does not automatically assert sixfold truth, and selecting a form does not rewrite the underlying documents.

### D. Work with an agent in the same QL-shaped construction

**Existing stories:** AG01, AG03, AG06, AG09; KN08; WR03, WR04; QL UX01, UX06, UX08. **Decisions:** WC08, WC09.

The person asks Epii to organise the selected material around a question. The actual agent receives sufficient permitted passages and current working state, proposes placements or connections and expresses its work in the shared field. The person edits a role, challenges an interpretation or replaces a passage, and the agent continues from the new working revision.

The existing Context area distinguishes what was selected from what the agent actually received. It uses compact exact-reference items, not an intrusive participant modal. The agent can point to a member, role or relation structurally and operate it through the same native Actions as the human.

A stale agent proposal cannot overwrite a newer human construction. Hold/interruption preserves meaningful work. Lens switching does not create a new agent identity, cancel unrelated work or disclose every source. Reversible composition can proceed within granted bounds; source changes and publication keep their actual native review requirements.

### E. Compose and inhabit the knowledge expressively

**Existing stories:** KN02, KN07; UI01, UI02, UI05, UI06; QL UX04, UX06, UX09. **Decisions:** WC06, WC07, WC14.

The person selects glyphs, lays out material in 3D, introduces text and images, and chooses line or particle presentations for declared relations. Form anchors preserve intelligibility while physical material can move. A rich presentation and the quiet Wiki graph identify the same whole and relationships without requiring identical layouts.

M2′ opens relevant relationship/becoming views, M3′ orders actual Expression Scenes, M4′ situates relevant places or occasions, and M5′ composes Expressions, constellations and Journeys into a Palace. These are available tools, not a mandatory six-stage wizard. Source/verso portals open exact material and return to the composition.

No lens creates a second renderer/Scene store. A capture is not live state; a configuration checkpoint is not exact particle rewind. Generic Expressions and ordinary Wiki use remain useful without QL.

### F. Return what was made and find it again

**Existing stories:** KN01, KN05, KN06, KN07, KN08, KN09; PX05; WR05; QL UX05, UX08, UX09. **Decisions:** WC09, WC10, WC14.

The person saves the constellation and intended typed QL relationships, produces a new explanatory artifact and finds all of them through ordinary Wiki/search/Library navigation. The returned whole retains members and contextual roles; it is not reduced to a pairwise clique. Sources retain their original links unless deliberately edited.

The agent reports what each native owner actually saved, with exact revisions and derivation. A saved Expression alongside a failed relation write is partial success, not a completed Return. Retry completes the missing work without duplicating earlier outputs. Generated material remains attributable to its source basis rather than pretending to supply independent corroboration.

The person can hide an overlay, retract a relation, revise a constellation or fork an alternative without confusing those acts. Re-entry can inspect what was originally read and what later changed.

### G. Leave, resume and encounter changed sources

**Existing stories:** KN05, KN08, KN09; UI02, UI07; AG06; QL UX11. **Decisions:** WC05, WC12, WC14.

The person switches lens, mode or workspace and returns to the same reading and construction. Released views reconstruct from retained models; restart restores useful work before unrelated tree enumeration. The initial visible page does not wait for remote providers or the entire corpus.

A source changes externally while a view is closed. Only affected link occurrences, memberships/readings and composition bindings become stale or require review. Unchanged references and authored layouts survive. A pinned historical reading remains historical; live work offers deliberate rebase. Dirty drafts and pending proposals remain recoverable.

Measure first usable content, native read counts, source/revision truth, layout work and resource growth. Do not call a stored tab title continuity when the writing or construction has been lost.

### H. Share a selected result without leaking the private field

**Existing stories:** SH01, SH02; AG03; QL UX12. **Decisions:** WC10, WC11.

The person publishes or shares an eligible result through the existing projection boundary. The admitted source/constellation/Expression refs remain navigable in the shared reading; private sources, agent context and excluded members do not enter its binding or counts. Local work continues without the shared provider.

Audience filtering precedes context expansion and cache delivery. Publication is a separate act from saving local composition. This extends existing SharedField behaviour; it is not a prerequisite to complete the local creative loop.

## 4. First integrated walk

Use an ordinary linked Markdown collection with no QL preparation. Read, preview and follow backlinks; filter the quiet graph; select exact passages; construct a constellation by each starting route; collaborate with a real agent on the same editable state; make glyph/text/image/line/particle presentation in the existing 3D Stage; create real Scenes and Palace composition; save and return QL relations and an artifact; rediscover them in the Wiki; resume after mode switch and restart; revise a source and inspect only the affected work.

Reuse one source in a second constellation. Exercise a stale concurrent agent edit, a failed relation save after successful presentation save, ambiguous/unresolved links, a slow/offline provider, and denied/private material. The full cases are WCT01–WCT18 in the Wayfinder. Native controlled checks, actual provider/material observations and human experience retain their distinct evidence grades.

## 5. UX source integration

`wiki-constellation.json` maps these activities to existing stories and required source obligations through `campaign.json` and `scripts/experience_map.py`. It preserves original story meanings, native matrices and all existing #65/C0–C5 obligations. QL's native crosswalk retains UX01–UX12 and their original source standing; these are additional cases, not replacements.

The source compiler can prove source coverage and references. It cannot prove that the agent loaded the right context, a relation was saved, the app was fast or a person found the experience useful. Those observations belong to the implementation return.
