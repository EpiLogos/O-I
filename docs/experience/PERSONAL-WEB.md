# Inhabit, craft and share a personal web

**Place:** vision-level intended experience, source-grounded elaboration under the owner-approved [Personal Web #279](https://github.com/EpiLogos/O-I/issues/279). The exact family names are **Beings** and **Things**. This is an additive source module of the one [#65 campaign](README.md), not a new runtime, test registry or reinstall programme. The [feature contract](../cradle/PERSONAL-WEB.md) carries full scope and source provenance; the table below is a set of experienced journeys, not its replacement.

The person can craft pages and a world with agents, organise through time and relations, and share selected parts. Six initial forms—Beings, Things, Flow/Dialogue, Day/4+2, Yoshimoto Cube and Epi-Card—supply a starting repertoire, not a ceiling. This is base O:I; Hen specialises native facilities later. C0 source/world, C1–C4 subject organisation and C5 presentation are constitutive relations, not privacy flags or a forced one-renderer-per-coordinate enum.

Keep all inherited 113 stories, 56 original obligation records, CAW and deep QL scope. The local #65 operator binds the actual source/build/installed/registered/running and practice-loading cut during the already planned reinstall; no feature is certified by story compilation.
