# Use the system in an existing life and working setup

## 0 — Why this campaign exists

**Campaign owner: [O:I #65](https://github.com/EpiLogos/O-I/issues/65).** This is the expanded story and operating map for that existing whole-product acceptance programme. It folds in CAW, installation/adoption, knowledge, agency, machines, developer work, sharing and the already-published QL experience. It is not a replacement for their design authorities, native implementation Wayfinders or evidence stores.

**Publication scope:** source-grounded planning and a locally executable testing procedure commissioned by the user. The detailed test stories are authored elaborations to be reviewed against their cited sources. Publication is not observed product behavior or the person's assessment. The separate QL UX synthesis remains at its own recorded H-ratification standing.

**Current installation authority:** [#268's 1+6 composition lock](https://github.com/EpiLogos/O-I/issues/268). Its [concrete story variants](INSTALLATION-VARIANTS.md) and `campaign.json` supersede earlier interpretations of #192 as the current installation taxonomy. Preserve existing story IDs and earlier test evidence; do not reinterpret old receipts or restart completed work.

The person already has applications, notes, projects, instructions, accounts, machines and ways of working. The intended benefit is that relevant information and useful agent practice accompany that work without constant restatement or supervision. The person can continue in familiar tools, use selected O:I facilities directly, or open richer interfaces when useful. Installing the system must not mean agreeing to use every product, fill a daily form, move every file, or route every conversation through Factory.

Examples of the thing to prove:

> I open my normal coding tool and ask for a fix. It finds the applicable project decision, works in the right place, runs the required check and shows the actual change. I never open the O:I desktop.
>
> I find an old research conclusion, follow its sources and discover what changed since it was written. I ask for a revised account without letting an agent rewrite my own notes.
>
> I create an assistant for this collection, choose what it may read and do, try it on a real task, revise it and speak to it again later.
>
> I ask one operator to update the development system safely. When something fails, it explains the actual choice and helps me continue the original work rather than handing me a new coordination job.

The user's latest correction also requires the **agent's side of each activity**: what brings a practice into relevance, what context determines the appropriate next act, which capabilities are needed, how the agent discovers them, and how failure becomes a recoverable situation. A tester must be able to locate a failed handoff, not merely report that a story-shaped script returned nonzero.

Parallelise independent test worlds, read-only investigations, platform specimens and disjoint owner files. ## 5 — What closure means

A planning check can establish valid identifiers, source locators, inheritance, method-description mechanics, complete old-ID inclusion and no dangling bindings. It cannot show that the system did the useful work.

A code/contract check establishes only the exercised behavior. A computer-use walk must actually take the user-facing route rather than invoke an internal helper while displaying screenshots. An agent-native walk must find and use the tools/practices normally available for that task. Both share the same underlying subject/effect evidence.

The parent remains open while required stories are unbound, unimplemented, unexercised or blocked. A bounded success records its selected composition and platform. Independent full-path proof plus applicable human assessment supports **usable end-to-end feature.** for that scope, not automatically the whole suite. The QL source's H-ratification and its actual human experience remain separate from this planning publication.

The local handoff should say what the person can now do, what remains awkward/broken, what the operator repaired, and the next real action. Technical refs remain behind that account. Never ask the person to reconstruct a failed story from raw logs or coordinate the native-owner sequencing themselves.
