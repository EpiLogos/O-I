## 0. The spine: the everyday loop

Every experiential condition here is settled by **operating the application**. Code, tests and contracts in other repositories prepare a condition; the running application and its actual native operations establish technical use, and the person assesses the human experience. Supporting evidence keeps that distinction. Repository implementation does not wait for access to the person's machine, and a passing repository test does not certify that machine.

A report of finished work is its author's idea of "plausibly done": a hypothesis, never an acceptance. Prose summaries, test output and contract tests do not establish a UX condition; they only justify trying the walk. Before work counts as done, whoever accepts it checks its meaning against the story and the owner's intent, walks the exact condition in the running app from a cold start, and runs the build and tests itself. If the work contradicts the design, the design wins: the work is fixed, or the conflict goes to the owner with reasons. A design document is never edited to match code.

The app is verified when, from a cold start on a machine with a Central ground — no environment variables, no handoff files, no development setup — a person can run the whole everyday loop:

> write something → bring Agency to it → the Agency inhabits the Worlds it
> legitimately needs → the person sees what it is actually doing → knowledge and
> context come into view when summoned → the work escalates into Factory when it
> becomes structured development → the material body stays implicit → work
> returns → the person recognises, refuses, or revises.

When that loop runs natively end to end, the spine holds. The conditions below state it per area, so that partial reality is always precisely located. Direct work remains complete without Factory. Central is the root meta-Project from which child Projects can be encountered; root work is not a failed child-Project lookup or an excuse to fabricate a Project binding.

The person is trying to work and to learn how to inhabit the system. They may write in their Day, converse through Flow/Dialogue, enter a native harness, commission development, or ask the root Agent to help. They should not have to restate their intention at each boundary, inspect implementation jargon to find a repair, supervise every Agent turn, or manually relay dependencies between five coding sessions.

The developer is also that person. Updating the suite must return them to their writing and work with a clear account of what changed. Rebase, squash, build, installation, projection and process restart are work the authorised operator carries and explains; they are not a puzzle the human must solve before testing. The full-suite developer acceptance cut is distinct from the minimal everyday composition. Missing an optional product must not prevent ordinary writing.

### 0.3 Conditions per area

These are functional conditions. They carry over to whichever shell hosts the work; the legacy Cradle's layout does not.

**Inhabitation.** The app starts from whatever exists. Recognition reads the live machine — installed harnesses, tools, daemons — and the composition reading names exactly what is present, absent, and degraded, as observed. Stopping a daemon or renaming a binary degrades the affected reading in place; identity is intact; nothing reports failure that is only absence. Root/meta-Project work and profile-less, legitimately situated participants are first-class. An unreadable or withheld relation is not evidence of absence or permission to widen.

**Focus.** Selecting a subject anywhere — file, knowledge node, agent, run, contribution — sets the one application-wide subject. Every view that can use a subject uses the same one; there is no component-local semantic selection. Opening the subject in another pane or window, splitting it and returning it all preserve the identical subject.

**Writing.** Blank writing is first-class, with no mandatory form completion. A blank page is not automatically a Flow. Day is the person's dated writing; Flow and Dialogue are views of the same entries, while the supplied Journal collection retains its own source meaning. No visual choice invents a semantic document or creates a second mutable copy. Commissioning sends only the authorised selection/context without changing human text. Returned material first has its actual receipt/proposal standing; reviewed inclusion makes a distinct contribution. Human edits to an H contribution become protected human revisions. Native acknowledged persistence and portable Save HTML copy remain different operations; a close warning is not autosave. The exact seventeen Day keys, CT4b fixtures, media/annotation IDs, no-script round-trip and 12 MB attachment limit remain source-defined.

**Agency.** An Agent may be created from a stated intent, or an existing Agent may already participate through an admissible World relation without a Central Profile. Actuation owns identity/Agency/WorldBinding; Central supplies authored residence and configuration; AIKit resolves scoped participation and operative context. Inspect actual determinations before acting. A visible participant, profile, SkillSet or mode does not supply authority. Presence and waiting are shown as they actually are, never as invented liveness. Skill use can produce evidence for later praxis/Routine proposals; admission retains its actual owner and required human authority. Method is a Skill whose description starts `METHOD:`, not a second ontology or a completion status.

**Encounter.** Conversation streams and can be interrupted; concurrent sessions do not freeze the app. A permission request names the exact subject and scope. The same session opened through another supported Surface retains identity and actual encounter provenance. Recorded history, prepared operative context and provider continuation state stay distinguishable. Native resume, rehydration and new attempt/fork are disclosed as what happened. A gateway append or transport ACK cannot prove another Agent received or completed work. Addressing a set does not collapse membership, invocation, authority or Attention; private material requires an explicit eligible sharing relation before it enters a group.

**Context and knowledge.** Selection can summon a permitted knowledge reading; it does not authorise context disclosure to an Agent. A knowledge view exposes the node, sources and history without forking identity. Show eligible/disclosed/excluded/withheld and relevant reasons without leaking protected identities. A human's Remember-this operation selects a destination and retains generated provenance. A proposal toward human-authored Ground requires its acceptance; Agent-owned operational knowledge follows its native bounded write/return law, not a blanket conversion of all knowledge to human-authored material. No source change, open, index refresh or clock boundary automatically invokes contemplation.

**Development.** Direct work remains complete without Factory. An explicit Commission admits structured development; it is not secretly created by a conversation or by opening a Run view. Journey direction, evidence/closure conditions and constraints persist into actual attempts. Candidate worktrees use the declared source basis for retrieval, cwd, edits, tests and returned diff. The dirty human checkout and other Candidates remain intact. Show alternative Candidates with their own Claims/Evidence, contradictions and independent review lineage. Comparing, recognising and revision-checked integration are separate. Semantic, Live, Trajectory and, when available, Cognitive are distinct readings of the same work, not a universal log store or an automatic performance score.

**Material.** An ordinary local Workcell is a valid default. Deliberate placement and working-environment requirements retain their strength through dispatch and recovery. A requirement cannot become an undisclosed preference; a permitted fallback remains attributable. NOW allocation, supported harness interception and material confinement establish different facts. Unsupported protection cannot be labelled enforced. Opening a Surface does not duplicate work; closing it does not cancel work.

**Shared.** A subject can be projected — audience, purpose, source revision — without transferring ownership; the projection carries its provenance line. Contributions remain attributable and scope-limited. A2A uses the same authorised native dispatch/Return path for external peers, preserving provider-scoped task references without making them permanent conversation identities. With no live shared substrate, read-only honesty does not simulate participation.

**Presentation.** Every view that is opened can be dismissed without residue. Layout restoration preserves dirty buffers and selected subjects; new Activity never steals focus. Missing optional services remain local, static and actionable where a real operation exists, rather than creating an indefinite whole-app loader.

**Authority.** Non-effectful reading still obeys source access and disclosure scope; it does not confer mutation or invocation authority. An already-issued bounded grant can carry ordinary authorised engineering without repeated human ceremony. Changed source/grant/expiry is rechecked at effects. Human refusal is recorded and leaves safe work usable. Imported content and rendered contribution code are never privileged callers. A returned proposal is not an accepted change.

### 0.4 Agent-native parity and operative practice

At each point an authorised Agent can reconstruct the same **permitted** actuality as the human, with stable subjects, Activity, reasons and provenance. Complete structured state means complete within the caller's scope, not universal access to private World material.

The verification begins with the Agent, not after it has somehow completed the operation. Observe how a fresh participant finds the register/subject and intent, recognises the appropriate Skill or METHOD from its description, discovers the native operation and its input/authority prerequisites, reads the actual result, handles refusal and leaves a resumable Return. Supplying an implementation-only helper prompt that conceals failed discovery does not pass this part.

Use the maintained practice sources rather than reproduce them in every ticket:

- [suite operator](../../skills/suite-operator/SKILL.md): one cross-product operator, evidence-aware remote/local work, upgrade/repair and one handoff;
- [session strap](../../skills/central-session-strap/SKILL.md): root/Project orientation and routing to actual lifecycle practices;
- AIKit's existing `runtime-operation`, `profile-skillset` and `verification` capsules: operative context, admission, staged composition and independent proof;
- Factory's existing `factory-bounded-work` METHOD and repair/change/coordination references: commissioned development and returned evidence;
- the Central repertoire in [Central #164](https://github.com/EpiLogos/Central/issues/164): `central-lifecycle`, `central-engineering`, `central-skills-system`, with atomic orientation, intake, retrieval, placement, NOW, ledger, contribution, archive, git-convergence, delegation, capability-discovery and release practices.

The private repertoire's current text and installed bindings must be inspected locally; an issue report is not a substitute for those bodies. Do not create a second repertoire here. AIKit remains owner of Skill/METHOD registration, classification, selection, trust, SkillSet composition and projection. Native products can author their own procedure sources; O:I does not register foreign Skills in a parallel manifest.

A source exists → is catalogued → is selected/eligible → is projected → is linked at the harness entry → is actually loaded/used are separate observations. Test a newly added Skill as well as a changed existing Skill. A changed symlink or generation does not prove that an already-running Agent reread its body. The existing AIKit #299 and #296 obligations belong in this acceptance rather than becoming hidden setup steps for the human.

## 1. Your job and why it exists

Make it possible for the person to use their own tools and files with reliable help from agents. Verify each selected activity through the normal human and agent entry, locate failures precisely, repair authorised defects at the native owner and repeat the real activity. The desired output is usable work and an understandable account, not a collection of green names.

You are the **one campaign orchestrator**. Product ownership determines where a repair belongs; it does not require the person to send messages between product chats. Reuse existing native tests, CAW P01–P28, TUI/desktop walks, provider conformance and QL checks. Do not build a second scheduler, test-result database, Skill registry or runtime.

Keep one concise current entry under #65 linking the exact round, active test assignments, current source/installed cut, real blockers, original failures and next steps. Native code/contract fixes and their evidence remain with the owner issues. Day/NOW entries point to these sources rather than retell the entire investigation.

The person's corrections that set this campaign's scope stay binding: ordinary existing tools first; benefit without opening O:I is a complete way to use it; full installation choices; granular agent creation; knowledge, graph and intelligence work as real work; authoring governance and context; actual machine setup; subagents and computer use for testing; and one local orchestrator, never the person relaying between coding chats. They authorise this campaign and its paired human and agent reading, not arbitrary changes to product defaults.

Your first reply after inspecting the current state names a small useful batch and begins it. It does not hand the person a new scheduling problem.

## 5. When a subagent or seam goes wrong

### Preserve before repair

Pause the affected action or use its real cancellation path. Do not kill unrelated sessions. Save the initial conditions, current revisions, actor/tool inputs, observed outputs and last confirmed effect. Classify whether effects are absent, confirmed or uncertain before retrying. Retain the original failed episode even when repair is easy.

### Locate the failure

Ask, in order only as needed:

1. Could the actor discover the correct World/root/Project and requested subject?
2. Was the relevant source allowed, current, sufficiently detailed and actually supplied?
3. Did an available description correctly trigger the fitting practice, or did wording/context misroute it?
4. Was the selected Skill/set actually present at the harness entry and loaded for this act?
5. Did the actor invoke the real native operation with the right subject, revision, scope and parameters?
6. Did credentials, authority, provider, material placement or connectivity prevent or alter execution?
7. Did the effect occur but disappear from the user's view, destination, context or later session?
8. Is the expected result/observer/test setup itself wrong or too narrow?

Bind the finding to **story + step + native capability + practice/context basis + actual evidence**. Use a plain description such as “the instruction was updated on disk, but the new agent could not discover it” before the technical detail. A model's self-report is evidence of its report, not automatically the cause.

### Repair at the right level

A misleading Skill trigger is a procedure/interface repair, not necessarily a missing API. A wrong context scope is a resolver/source-policy issue. An unavailable provider is not fixed by editing a test to return success. A lost task/receipt boundary is fixed in the native caller/receiver, not by a new campaign ledger. A wrong expectation is corrected with source-backed explanation; an unresolved product decision goes to the person once, clearly.

The orchestrator may allocate a bounded repair to the relevant owner and integrate it through the current branch/review/check discipline. It may use Factory if operationally available and appropriate, but needing Factory to build the ability to test Factory would be circular. The approved local/native/GitHub procedure remains an explicit bootstrap path. No recursive unlimited delegation or retry is implied.

### Repeat fairly

After repair, establish the new source/build/installed/running/practice basis. Restore comparable input from a safe baseline and rerun through the normal entry with a fresh agent where context/learning could contaminate the result. If the actor was coached, label that attempt assisted and repeat unassisted. A repaired continuation can succeed while the original failure remains recorded. Never delete the failing assertion or downgrade an unmet parent requirement to pass.

Repeated trials with a model declare their changes of input, seed, model or context, and any sampling rule, before success is read from them. Keep the failures and the variance. Do not pick the first good answer or invent a universal performance score.

## 13. Optimise useful work, and the process of improving it

Optimisation is carried through the existing rounds of this protocol under [O:I #65](https://github.com/EpiLogos/O-I/issues/65); [founding positions](../../ProjectCentral/docs/0-ground/positions/FOUNDING-POSITIONS.md) remain upstream. #220 retains connective implementation; native owners retain their code and evidence. This is not a second campaign, scheduler, model registry or results database. Preserve all existing stories, CAW P01–P28 and named cases, developer/gateway/SDK obligations and the complete delegated QL field.

### 13.1 Purpose and invariant

Improve the quality, reliability, responsiveness or economy of an intended activity while preserving its meaning, authority, scope and required fidelity. The system should become better at discovering, testing, retaining and applying improvements—not merely generate more lessons or use fewer tokens.

Capability, cost, latency, reliability, human attention and learning are distinct dimensions. Compare useful operating points for the actual undertaking, not a universal intelligence score. More expensive or slower work can be the appropriate improvement when it establishes a necessary result, preserves reflective depth or enables a richer experience. Reducing scope, concealing failures or silently lowering numerical/expressive fidelity does not establish optimisation of the same activity.

Privacy, source/provenance, identity, authority, correctness, independent verification and consequential human choice constrain every comparison. Never reward agreement with a system interpretation as proof of human benefit. Ordinary Direct work, partial installations and no-QL operation remain complete choices.

### 13.2 Eight lanes of awareness

These are measurement/diagnosis dimensions, not new product positions or eight mandatory reports per task. At C0 select the relevant lanes; retain an explicit reason when a material concern is deferred or cannot be measured.

| Lane | Improvement sought | Candidate observations and failure questions |
|---|---|---|
| Capability and outcome | More complete, repeatable useful work | Independent success and retained scope; accepted outcomes over all attempts; significant errors; transfer to unfamiliar inputs; did a cheap result omit the difficult requirement? |
| Agent economics and orchestration | Inference and coordination earn their cost | Whole-task input/output/cache usage and spend; failed attempts, retries, verification and synthesis; time to accepted result; single/plural agency and escalation; did delegation add duplicated work or correlated mistakes? |
| Context, knowledge and praxis | Sufficient permitted material and appropriate practice become operative | Governing-source retrieval, freshness, relevance, discovery/trigger precision and near misses; selected/projected/actually loaded revisions; repeated context reconstruction; did the agent lack a clear native operation rather than a stronger model? |
| Runtime and material | Responsive operation under actual resource constraints | Startup, interaction/cancel latency, frame-time distributions, audio deadlines, memory/VRAM growth, CPU/GPU/IO and idle work where measurable; queues, thermal/energy conditions where available; lifecycle leaks and foreground contention |
| Data and distributed worlds | Correct incremental change without excess movement or disclosure | Index rebuild versus incremental work; payloads, subscriptions and cache reuse; source/generation age; reconnect and convergence; distinguish coalescible presentation updates from consequential commands, source edits and Returns |
| Repairability and maintainability | Safe recovery and fewer recurring defects | Time to independently verified restoration, uncertain effects, rollback success, repeat incidents, unnecessary architectural complexity, upgrades and compatibility; native-owner fix versus installed workaround |
| Human attention and experience | Less supervision and friction with depth nearby | Restatement, avoidable interruptions, assistance, loss of focus/continuity, accessibility, explanation and actual human assessment; do not infer H/EX from screenshots or agent approval |
| Learning and optimisation quality | Experience produces transferable, worthwhile improvement | Useful later uptake, held-out gains, recurrence/regressions, rejected experiments, stale/duplicate skills, time and resources spent improving, and the fitness of the improvement procedure itself |

Instrumentation has a cost and disclosure boundary. Measure its overhead where material; sample or defer expensive observation without silently removing safety checks. Missing measurements are unknown, not zero. A small source task should not trigger a whole-suite census or an LLM reflection by default.

### 13.4 Whole-undertaking economics and fair comparison

The unit of economic comparison is verified useful work, not a cheap turn. Keep all attempts, failed/rejected candidates, retries, cancelled or late work, synthesis, verification, repair and the attributable learning cost. Report provider input/output/cache-read/cache-write or other usage only when exposed, with model/variant/provider/harness/settings and evidence. Avoid double counting across parent/child correlations or aggregate and raw receipts.

Distinguish provider-reported spend from an estimate with currency, pricing source/revision and calculation basis. Subscription/quota constraints and local resource use are not fabricated per-token cash charges. Human intervention/time stays visible separately. Cost per accepted outcome must name the denominator, task set and acceptance test; with no accepted outcomes it is undefined, never free. Capability/risk/coverage and failure rates remain beside cost.

Before each experiment state the hypothesis, quality/fidelity floor, eligible alternatives, metric definitions, material budgets, intended comparison and stopping rule. Preserve exact source/build/install/running/Skill/context/model/provider/engine/quantisation/parameter/hardware conditions where relevant. Record cold/warm cache, load, seeds and nondeterminism. Version changing provider aliases honestly when exact internals are unavailable.

Use comparable realistic inputs and repeat observations sufficiently for the decision and risk. Report sample counts, variation and uncertainty; a single successful specimen does not establish general superiority. Paired or interleaved comparisons can reduce time/load confounding. Compare both equal-budget outcomes and resource use at the same required quality where useful. Do not change several conditions and attribute all improvement to the model. Diagnose discovery, tool/API, context/loading, authority, dispatch/material, result and verifier defects separately.

Working actors receive normal tasks and permitted discovery routes, not verifier answers, hidden filenames or canaries. Keep development/evaluation/held-out episodes separate; candidate training and review must not consume the holdout answers. Qualify correlated reviewers and independent lineage. Automated judgement is evidence at its tested scope, not human EX.

### 13.6 Learning, repair and admission

Use the smallest intervention that addresses the diagnosed cause: a source/description fix, retrieval/loading repair, native tool/API improvement, bounded composition change, executable helper or Skill revision. Stable verified deterministic operations need not be rediscovered through inference at every use. Do not compensate for unclear product interfaces with an ever larger prompt.

Repair restores an intended condition. Learning proposes a future practice/capability. Promotion makes the change operative or authoritative. These transitions can be related without sharing permission. Bounded automated experiments and safe repairs use existing grants; consequential source, policy and capability changes retain native approval and Recognition. Neither recurrence nor a learned success replenishes consumed authority.

A saved lesson is a candidate. Validate its trigger and near misses, supported inputs, effects, failure/recovery and source lineage. Then prove normal discovery and actual loading by a fresh agent, followed by useful reuse on unfamiliar inputs. Replaying the original output or showing a skill file is insufficient. Preserve a worked example, explanation, applicability, disconfirmations and retirement/rollback. Rare recovery or authority-boundary practice is not worthless merely because it is infrequent.

Triage reflection opportunities by novelty, repeated failure, correction and expected future use; include reflection cost. Compare this policy with alternatives rather than mandate an expensive review after every trivial act. Consolidation must preserve supporting scripts/assets/references and critical capabilities. Usage frequency informs maintenance but does not confer trust or silently author a preference.

Hermes is a named comparative case for this work: inspect its then-current source for skill capture/review/maintenance, foreground-resource interaction and recoverable retirement. Record the exact revision and compare actual mechanisms/results; do not import a second memory/skill owner, assume its defaults fit O:I, or treat its feature descriptions as measured gains here.
