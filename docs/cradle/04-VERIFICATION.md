# 04 — Verification

**Status:** the conditions that hold when the Cradle exists as designed.
**Companions:** [01-DESIGN.md](01-DESIGN.md) (intent), [02-ARCHITECTURE.md](02-ARCHITECTURE.md) (structure), [03-UX-STATES.md](03-UX-STATES.md) (states).

## 1. What verification means here

Every experiential condition in this document is settled by **operating the application**.
Code, tests, and contracts in other repositories prepare a condition; the
running application and its actual native operations establish technical use,
and the person assesses the human experience. Supporting evidence must retain
that distinction. Repository implementation does not wait for access to the
person's machine, and a passing repository test does not certify that machine.

## 2. The spine: the everyday loop

The app is verified when, from a cold start on a machine with a Central ground —
no environment variables, no handoff files, no development setup — a person can
run the whole loop of the design (01 §11):

> write something → bring Agency to it → the Agency inhabits the Worlds it
> legitimately needs → the person sees what it is actually doing → knowledge and
> context come into view when summoned → the work escalates into Factory when it
> becomes structured development → the material body stays implicit → work
> returns → the person recognises, refuses, or revises.

When that loop runs natively end to end, the Cradle exists. The sections below
state the conditions per area, so that partial reality is always precisely
located. Direct work remains complete without Factory. Central is the root
meta-Project from which child Projects can be encountered; root work is not a
failed child-Project lookup or an excuse to fabricate a Project binding.

[bootstrap and loading contract](../../.superpowers/sdd/cradle-rebuild/BOOTSTRAP-AND-LOADING.md).
Issues and authored design, not pull-request descriptions, establish these
obligations. Implementation and observed use can reveal discrepancies, not
retroactively define the intended experience.

The person is trying to work and to learn how to inhabit the system. They may
write in their Day, converse through Flow/Dialogue, enter a native harness,
commission development, or ask the root Agent to help. They should not have to
restate their intention at each boundary, inspect implementation jargon to find
a repair, supervise every Agent turn, or manually relay dependencies between
five coding sessions.

The developer is also that person. Updating the suite must return them to their
writing and work with a clear account of what changed. Rebase, squash, build,
installation, projection and process restart are work the authorised operator
carries and explains; they are not a puzzle the human must solve before testing.
The full-suite developer acceptance cut is distinct from the minimal everyday
composition. Missing an optional product must not prevent ordinary writing.

## 3. Conditions per area

**Inhabitation.** The app starts from whatever exists. With no World configured
it offers one act: write, or recognise this machine. Recognition reads the live
machine — installed harnesses, tools, daemons — and the composition reading
names exactly what is present, absent, and degraded, as observed. Stopping a
daemon or renaming a binary degrades the affected reading in place; identity is
intact; nothing reports failure that is only absence. Root/meta-Project work
and profile-less, legitimately situated participants are first-class. An
unreadable or withheld relation is not evidence of absence or permission to widen.

**Focus.** Selecting a subject anywhere — file, knowledge node, agent, run,
contribution — sets the one application-wide subject. Every surface that can use
a subject uses the same one; there is no component-local semantic selection.
Promoting a summoned surface to the centre, splitting it, detaching it to a
window, and returning it, all preserve the identical subject.

**Writing.** Blank writing is first-class, with no mandatory form completion.
The current file-opening and document-choice contract in the Wave 6 Wayfinder
refines the earlier shorthand that every blank canvas is automatically a Flow.
Day is the person's dated writing; Flow and Dialogue are views of the same
entries, while the supplied Journal collection retains its own source meaning.
No visual choice invents a semantic document or creates a second mutable copy.
Commissioning sends only the authorised selection/context without changing
human text. Returned material first has its actual receipt/proposal standing;
reviewed inclusion makes a distinct contribution. Human edits to an H
contribution become protected human revisions. Native acknowledged persistence
and portable Save HTML copy remain different operations; a close warning is not
autosave. The exact seventeen Day keys, CT4b fixtures, media/annotation IDs,
no-script round-trip and 12 MB attachment limit remain source-defined.

**Agency.** An Agent may be created from a stated intent, or an existing Agent
may already participate through an admissible World relation without a Central
Profile. Actuation owns identity/Agency/WorldBinding; Central supplies authored
residence and configuration; AIKit resolves scoped participation and operative
context. Inspect actual determinations before acting. A visible participant,
profile, SkillSet or mode does not supply authority. The left map shows actual
presence and actionable waiting, not invented liveness. Skill use can produce
evidence for later praxis/Routine proposals; admission retains its actual owner
and required human authority. Method is a Skill whose description starts
`METHOD:`, not a second ontology or a completion status.

**Encounter.** Conversation streams and can be interrupted; concurrent sessions
do not freeze the app. A permission request names the exact subject and scope.
The same session opened through another supported Surface retains identity and
actual encounter provenance. Recorded history, prepared operative context and
provider continuation state stay distinguishable. Native resume, rehydration and
new attempt/fork are disclosed as what happened. A gateway append or transport
ACK cannot prove another Agent received or completed work. Addressing a set does
not collapse membership, invocation, authority or Attention; private material
requires an explicit eligible sharing relation before it enters a group.

**Context and knowledge.** Selection can summon a permitted knowledge reading;
it does not authorise context disclosure to an Agent. The aperture exposes the
node, sources and history without forking identity. Show eligible/disclosed/
excluded/withheld and relevant reasons without leaking protected identities.
A human's Remember-this operation selects a destination and retains generated
provenance. A proposal toward human-authored Ground requires its acceptance;
Agent-owned operational knowledge follows its native bounded write/return law,
not a blanket conversion of all knowledge to human-authored material. No source
change, open, index refresh or clock boundary automatically invokes contemplation.

**Development.** Direct work remains complete without Factory. An explicit
Commission admits structured development; it is not secretly created by a
conversation or by opening a Run view. Journey direction, evidence/closure
conditions and constraints persist into actual attempts. Candidate worktrees
use the declared source basis for retrieval, cwd, edits, tests and returned diff.
The dirty human checkout and other Candidates remain intact. Show alternative
Candidates with their own Claims/Evidence, contradictions and independent review
lineage. Comparing, recognising and revision-checked integration are separate.
Semantic, Live, Trajectory and, when available, Cognitive are distinct readings
of the same work, not a universal log store or an automatic performance score.

**Material.** An ordinary local Workcell is a valid default. Deliberate placement
and working-environment requirements retain their strength through dispatch and
recovery. A requirement cannot become an undisclosed preference; a permitted
fallback remains attributable. NOW allocation, supported harness interception
and material confinement establish different facts. Unsupported protection
cannot be labelled enforced. Opening a Surface does not duplicate work; closing
it does not cancel work. Deep administration remains summoned System/CLI depth.

**Shared.** A subject can be projected — audience, purpose, source revision —
without transferring ownership; the projection carries its provenance line.
Contributions remain attributable and scope-limited. A2A uses the same authorised
native dispatch/Return path for external peers, preserving provider-scoped task
references without making them permanent conversation identities. With no live
shared substrate, read-only honesty does not simulate participation.

**Presentation.** The resting shape is the agency field and canvas. Every
summoned depth dismisses without residue. Full workbench capability comes through
promotion, not a competing semantic destination system. Layout restoration
preserves dirty buffers and selected subjects; new Activity never steals focus.
Missing optional services remain local, static and actionable where a real
operation exists, rather than creating an indefinite whole-app loader.

**Authority.** Non-effectful reading still obeys source access and disclosure
scope; it does not confer mutation or invocation authority. An already-issued
bounded grant can carry ordinary authorised engineering without repeated human
ceremony. Changed source/grant/expiry is rechecked at effects. Human refusal is
recorded and leaves safe work usable. Imported content and rendered contribution
code are never privileged callers. A returned proposal is not an accepted change.

## 4. Agent-native parity and operative practice

At each point an authorised Agent can reconstruct the same **permitted**
actuality as the human, with stable subjects, Activity, reasons and provenance.
Complete structured state means complete within the caller's scope, not universal
access to private World material.

The verification begins with the Agent, not after it has somehow completed the
operation. Observe how a fresh participant finds the register/subject and intent,
recognises the appropriate Skill or METHOD from its description, discovers the
native operation and its input/authority prerequisites, reads the actual result,
handles refusal and leaves a resumable Return. Supplying an implementation-only
helper prompt that conceals failed discovery does not pass this part.

Use the maintained practice sources rather than reproduce them in every ticket:

- [suite operator](../../skills/suite-operator/SKILL.md): one cross-product
  operator, evidence-aware remote/local work, upgrade/repair and one handoff;
- [session strap](../../skills/central-session-strap/SKILL.md): root/Project
  orientation and routing to actual lifecycle practices;
- AIKit's existing `runtime-operation`, `profile-skillset` and `verification`
  capsules: operative context, admission, staged composition and independent proof;
- Factory's existing `factory-bounded-work` METHOD and repair/change/coordination
  references: commissioned development and returned evidence;
- the Central repertoire in [Central #164](https://github.com/EpiLogos/Central/issues/164):
  `central-lifecycle`, `central-engineering`, `central-skills-system`, with atomic
  orientation, intake, retrieval, placement, NOW, ledger, contribution, archive,
  git-convergence, delegation, capability-discovery and release practices.

The private repertoire's current text and installed bindings must be inspected
locally; an issue report is not a substitute for those bodies. Do not create a
second repertoire here. AIKit remains owner of Skill/METHOD registration,
classification, selection, trust, SkillSet composition and projection. Native
products can author their own procedure sources; O:I does not register foreign
Skills in a parallel manifest.

A source exists → is catalogued → is selected/eligible → is projected → is
linked at the harness entry → is actually loaded/used are separate observations.
Test a newly added Skill as well as a changed existing Skill. A changed symlink
or generation does not prove that an already-running Agent reread its body.
The existing AIKit #299 and #296 obligations belong in this acceptance rather
than becoming hidden setup steps for the human.

## 5. Build order

The original vertical sequence remains the experiential dependency order, not a
requirement to stop independent web coding until a physical machine is available.
Current execution uses #190's Wave 6–8 amendments and #220's one connective
session after bounded parallel first passes. Do not restart earlier completed
verticals or make the human route dependencies among product chats.

1. **Writing + World** — native source open/edit, root/Project ground, conflict,
   history, shared focus and faithful supplied documents.
2. **Agency encounter** — selected identity, actual dispatch/response, Activity,
   permission/interrupt and supported continuation through another Surface.
3. **Knowledge in action** — bounded source discovery, selective context,
   related material and provenance-bearing Return.
4. **Attention** — real requests/decisions over the same Activity and receiving
   path, leaving routine progress out of the human's way.
5. **Factory mode** — explicit Commission, real bounded work, Candidate evidence
   and separately attributable Recognition/integration.
6. **Material** — implicit/default and intentional placement, enforcement,
   relocation and failure/recovery without identity loss.
7. **Shared** — the same native operation extended through supported external
   encounter, never a disconnected transport demonstration.

Write owner code and controlled tests wherever tools can execute them. Missing
upstream code blocks its particular join; the one connective session implements
it in the native owner. Commercial credentials and private installation are later
proof inputs, not coding prerequisites. A missing compiler is not a mandate to
build a compiler-transfer system. Missing source access is reported precisely.

## 6. Acceptance

The app is accepted when the everyday loop of §2 has been lived in — used for
real work over a sustained period on a physical machine — with the relevant
conditions of §3 holding throughout. Record what was done, the exact source and
installed/running composition, which World, what returned and what the person
actually validated. Keep specified, technically supported, exercised, failed,
blocked and human-validated separate in the existing ledger.

A fresh verification session or independent subagent that did not implement the
slice reads the original acceptance, exercises the full public operation and a
consequential failure/recovery case, and rejects a disconnected producer. Only
the fully supported parent may receive **usable end-to-end feature.** A child
merge, snapshot, receipt, screenshot or green subset cannot supply that verdict.

### 6.1 Human walk: arrive and understand before upgrading

**Start:** a real existing Central root/meta-Project, two child Projects, useful
uncommitted work, an open Day/Flow and a continuing Agent task. The selected
installation modality and current sources are known; no secrets enter evidence.

**Ask:** “Help me understand what I have, bring the development system up to date,
and let me continue this work. Explain the choices I actually need to make.”

**Agent:** distinguish source, built artifact, installed registration, resident
process, effective Skill generation and loaded context. Discover relevant native
status/doctor/inspect operations and show only the reasons that affect this goal.
Offer Teach/Do with/Do for under existing bounds. Account for all seven product
lines for this developer campaign without making all seven mandatory for writing.

**Observe/control:** the person sees what remains usable, what would change,
what is held for active work, the rollback boundary, and real choices of
proceed/defer/refuse. They do not have to choose repository merge order or copy
messages between Agents. Cancelling leaves the original work and drafts intact.

**Failure:** a clean Git status is called an upgraded running system; an optional
absence blocks writing; technical identifiers replace an explanation; or the
operator asks the human to repair its dependency sequencing.

### 6.2 Human walk: update, encounter a failure and recover

**Start:** the reviewed developer-source change from 6.1. Each acceptance worktree
is isolated from unrelated dirty work. The operator has the relevant explicit
permissions; a request to update is not authority to rewrite shared main/history.

**Act:** approve the scoped update. The operator reconciles required branches and
squash/rebase ancestry, retains unique work, runs verification on the final
accepted revisions, builds and registers the coherent suite including companions,
and safely drains/restarts or preserves resident services on their actual version.
Use isolated build outputs or serialised builds so worktrees cannot supply stale
binaries to one another. Pin one campaign cut rather than chase moving main.

**Failure exercise:** with a disposable approved specimen, supply a stale binary,
failed activation or interrupted service transition. Show the first actual failed
relation, preserved/uncertain effects, usable unaffected surfaces and a real
repair/rollback route. Retrying must not repeat an uncertain consequential effect.

**Finish:** the same document/work is reopened; current authority and actual
context are re-established, not inferred from old chat text. New source and
loaded practice are distinguishable. The Return says what changed, what stayed,
what remains and where the evidence is. A repair changing the cut reruns affected
proof; rollback of code is not a claim to undo irreversible data effects.

**Failure:** personal files reset for cleanliness, unsupported confinement called
safe, silent old-process use, automatic human-source rewrite, or fresh work
disguised as resumed work. Technical recovery is insufficient until the person
can continue the original task.

### 6.3 Human walk: write, address and learn the system

Open the actual Day or Flow/Dialogue file. Leave some fields blank. Write a
question, select a passage and ask an existing root Agent to help. Before Send,
inspect the recipient, selected material, applicable context and bounds; passive
selection neither sends nor begins Factory work. The Agent finds and uses its
relevant native practice rather than needing the user to paste its procedure.
Repeat with an eligible profile-less participant and at child-Project scope.

Observe a real attributed response and its actual delivery state. Refuse a
permission request; another permitted activity remains usable. Change source
while a reply is in flight; stale inclusion must preserve both revisions.
Review/include the contribution and switch Flow/Dialogue without losing entry,
media or annotation identity. Export/reopen the portable document, including
no-script content. Human writing, H contribution and acknowledged inclusion are
not merged into one source of truth.

### 6.4 Human walk: entrust development and compare what returned

From the writing, commission one real suite change with an evidence/closure
condition, bounded authority and an intentional environment requirement. The
root Agent can help compose the arrangement; its mode does not grant power.
Two existing Guardians act through different supported harnesses, with correctly
isolated Candidate worktrees and task NOWs. Independent verification consumes
actual changed artifacts. Keep a Direct session alive beside the Factory work.

Inspect semantic work, actual participants and exact trajectory; open the same
work elsewhere and detach without starting it again. Make one leg unavailable
after partial work. The failed/missing leg remains in the barrier and historical
results stay attributed; re-resolution preserves work and rechecks its constraints.
Compare distinct Candidates, including contradictory checks. A human's choice
is not yet a successful Git integration against a changed target.

Leave the app. Receive one clear pending Return through the Day from another
harness; reopen and review it. It states what changed, what remains and what
needs the person, with optional evidence depth. It does not tick personal tasks,
certify itself or demand that the human read raw logs to know the result.

### 6.5 Human walk: time, archive and a later participant

Keep a NOW task active across the configured civil Day boundary while writing
remains open. An authorised Routine can re-enter only under current proof and
authority. Duplicate trigger, late result, expired grant and changed praxis have
explicit outcomes. Midnight alone neither invokes a model nor completes work.

Archive only through the current owner protocol after accounting for pending
messages, permissions, uncertain effects, Returns and retention references.
A fresh Agent then resumes from the governing plan, exact source state and
bounded retained context. It can explain what is known and what is missing.
The human can reach original evidence from the compact reading. Summarisation
budgets govern operational ledger prose, never the person's Day or the depth of
plans/conversations. An archive pointer that cannot recover needed work fails.

### 6.6 Human walk: a practice actually reaches a new session

Select an existing native Skill/METHOD appropriate to the task through AIKit.
Inspect its real description, provenance, required tools and trust/target state.
In a disposable permitted World, introduce a harmless new practice and revise
an existing one. Follow the native source/selection/projection path, inspect the
harness entry/link, then observe an appropriately fresh session discovering and
performing it. Also remove/retire it without leaving an unmanageable reference.

Exercise missing owner capability, stale source and withheld trust. The operator
must explain exactly which stage is unavailable. A bounded temporary bridge may
preserve an owned link or explicitly load an exact authorised body only under its
recorded plan; it cannot counterfeit a native install, authority or hot reload.
The human should not discover a missing first-time link during unrelated work.

## 7. Source reconciliation and unresolved evidence

The original conversation supplies the negative requirements as well as positive
ones: “one prompt for the connective session”, “it's the user's day”, “agent in
situ”, and “clean squashed/rebased state across all product lines” are retained
as authorial anchors, not inferred from current code. The exact template specs
and linked issue amendments make their operational consequences inspectable.
The source handoffs about TUI and Agency are supporting records; their historical
implementation reports are not current proof.

This file repairs earlier shorthand in the spine: blank canvas does not force a
Flow; ordinary direct work does not imply a Commission; waiting does not always
mean a human must act; read-only is not unrestricted disclosure; returned is not
recognised. The companion state document records the same interpretations.
No architectural claim is inferred from a claimed phenomenal difference between
humans and models: Day/NOW is the user's authored interaction/ownership design.

Code/operation availability and dated observed defects belong to the current
#220/#201 readiness ledger and native issues. In particular, locally reported
Central #164 practice bodies and the personal installation cannot be certified
from repository reading. Required missing inputs are named before the human
