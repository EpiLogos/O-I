# Wiki, constellations and Technè: connected knowledge as creative practice

**Standing:** owner-directed product clarification and implementation-planning commission, 20 September 2026. This document preserves the owner's meaning and makes the resulting design decisions explicit. Explanatory elaboration is attributed to this design pass; it is not a claim of implemented behaviour or blanket ratification of older pending QL UX material.

**Upstream:** [Founding positions](FOUNDING-POSITIONS.md). **Development entry:** [joined Wayfinder](../../.wayfinder/maps/wiki-constellation-development.md). **Human/agent experience:** [UX spine extension](../experience/WIKI-CONSTELLATION-UX.md). **Contract detail:** [Cradle specification](../cradle/WIKI-CONSTELLATION-SPEC.md).

## 1. The owner clarification and its reason

The owner distinguished the ordinary Wiki's connected source material from the editable constellation organisation made over it. Markdown links, wikilinks, backlinks, tags and references are not preliminary demo data: they make existing writing and project knowledge available without requiring QL. Humans and agents then construct, discover, revise and recombine constellations in the QL frame. Technè operates through the real Expression system: three-dimensional composition, selectable glyphs, lines or particle relations, text-writing overlays, images, motion and physical material. The results become Expressions, further writing and artifacts, and Palace compositions of Expressions. Deliberately authored QL relationships can return to the underlying Wiki and enrich subsequent work.

The owner's further correction matters: an older implementation or an agent-written prohibition does not determine product meaning. In particular, an old "no cache" comment is not an architectural law, and a serial ten-item conversion is not the definition of expressing a constellation. The reported newer local mains are the implementation reconciliation target when pushed. They do not prevent this design from being made definite now.

This develops the founding commitment to meet heterogeneous existing Worlds. Someone should gain immediate value from their existing writing and then gain additional powers of organisation, interpretation and composition. They do not have to surrender their sources, adopt QL, or understand every instrument before reading and linking a document.

## 2. Two kinds of authorship in one connected field

The **source field** contains existing and newly authored documents, passages, media, links, properties, tags and other native knowledge relations. The Wiki is already creative: people write, connect and revise material there.

A **constellation** is a deliberately composed organisation of selected material in a particular inquiry or frame. Its members may be documents, passages, media, native subjects or other constellations. The organisation adds contextual roles, typed relations and an articulated whole. A source may participate in several constellations with different roles without changing its identity or being permanently classified by one interpretation.

These are cooperating semantic layers, not two Wikis, two databases or a mandatory import pipeline. The overlay describes the relation of authored organisation to source material. Once recorded, constellations and their attributable relationships are themselves discoverable native knowledge, distinguishable by kind and provenance inside the same field.

There are two equally valid starting acts:

- **Material-first:** gather connected material, investigate it, then articulate a constellation.
- **Frame-first:** choose a QL form and question, place material into it, expose missing positions, compare alternatives, and revise the construction.

Choosing a frame is an authored interpretive act. Formal validity, evidential support and human acceptance are different questions. The person may explore an unproven interpretation without the system either forbidding composition or presenting the interpretation as established knowledge.

## 3. Decisions made here

These are product and design decisions, not facts inferred from current code. Implementers must carry both each decision and its reason into their lane. A contrary technical requirement is returned as a specific amendment, not silently substituted.

| ID | Decision | Reason and human consequence |
|---|---|---|
| WC01 | First-class ordinary Markdown/Wiki use is a complete product path without QL. | Existing projects and writing already have useful structure. Import, read, edit, search and traverse them without forced QL classification or source conversion. |
| WC02 | Reader links, backlinks, tag membership, search and graph consume the same native resolution and relationship evidence. | A link must not mean different things in text, graph and agent context. Source occurrences and exact passage references remain reachable. |
| WC03 | Constellations are editable contextual organisations, not pictures of already-certified structures. | People and agents can make, revise and compare ideas. Both frame-first and material-first work are first-class. |
| WC04 | QL form validity does not establish the truth of a member assignment or relation. | A useful interpretive experiment may be formally sound and still contestable. Preserve explanation, evidence and standing without making every gesture an approval ceremony. |
| WC05 | Membership and role are constellation-scoped; composite wholes retain their own identities. | The same passage can serve different inquiries. Reuse, instance-specific presentation, deliberate variant and source revision must be distinguishable. |
| WC06 | Technè uses the full existing three-dimensional Expression composition medium. | Glyphs, text, images, lines, particles, motion and Scenes are operative making tools, not optional decoration on a graph screenshot. |
| WC07 | M0′ makes the corpus available for construction; M1′ deepens the construction tools. | The person can gather passages or initiate a constellation from the Wiki/graph and continue in the same field. Instrument switching is not a transfer to another app. |
| WC08 | The QL-shaped agent staging space is the shared working construction over exact corpus references. | Human corrections must change the object the agent is working on. It is not a hidden prompt-only model, a second corpus or a new agent runtime. |
| WC09 | Deliberate semantic authoring is supported; visual proximity alone does not assert a knowledge relation. | Drawing and typing an intended connection can create a native relation through the appropriate Action. Moving objects remains reversible composition. The ownership rule enables authoring instead of prohibiting it. |
| WC10 | Constellations, QL relations, Expressions and artifacts return to discovery through their existing native owners. | Work enriches the field. N-ary whole/member structure must survive; the system must not turn a constellation into an indiscriminate pairwise clique or generated evidence into independent corroboration. |
| WC11 | Filtering changes disclosure, not the meaning or membership of a constellation. | Show source links, authored QL relations, membership and presentation separately or together. A partially shown sixfold does not become a twofold merely because two members match. |
| WC12 | Kernel loading/resource state is a legitimate retained third responsibility between owner truth and presentation. | Shared readings, in-flight work, revision-aware indexes and working models survive disposable views. Ownership does not imply repeated cold reads. Use the existing state/continuity work, not a competing cache service. |
| WC13 | Wiki fluency includes strong search, filters, backlinks, previews, keyboard operation and durable return. | Adopt useful established interaction patterns confidently. Preserve the quiet small-node/thin-line Wiki basis while Technè exposes richer spatial making. |
| WC14 | Build one joined creation-and-return loop, with independently useful Wiki and Technè increments. | A good filter panel or a staged fixture is not the complete result. Neither instrument completion nor hosted publication gates unrelated local Wiki improvements. |

## 4. The constructive medium and agent practice

A bound source can be expressed as a glyph, text, image, particle formation or supported native carrier. A bound relation can be a thin line, particle stream or another declared expressive connection. Selecting it addresses the real member or relation. Authored anchors and formation constraints retain recognisable structure while permitted material behaviour expresses movement and tension.

The same constellation can have a quiet Wiki view and rich Expression presentations. Structural continuity means the same whole, members, contextual roles and authored connections; it does not require pixel-identical layouts. A free spatial arrangement is legitimate composition. A QL-bound arrangement should disclose the binding and any deliberate departure instead of relabelling arbitrary coordinates as a canonical shape.

The working constellation holds the question, selected references, current frame, provisional assignments, proposed relations, alternatives and outputs. Its source material remains in the corpus. Existing Agency, AIKit context/skills and AgentSession mechanisms make the work operative; `TechneReading`, `DisclosureSession` and existing Expression operations carry co-reference. Available corpus, selected material, actually disclosed context and agent proposals remain distinguishable.

The person can ask Epii to propose a structure, edit it directly, challenge a relation and continue from that revised construction. Bounded reversible presentation work proceeds under granted authority. Semantic saves, source edits and publication use their native Actions and applicable review, not a blanket modal for each selection.

## 5. Return makes the practice cumulative

A saved constellation remains an addressable whole. Its contextual role assignments and attributable QL relationships can be traversed in the Wiki. An Expression, Journey or Palace is discoverable composition over real references. A generated essay or image becomes new source material with derivation links. These returns have different owners and save operations, but can be encountered together.

A relation retains what was asserted, by whom or by which agent act, within which constellation/frame, on which source revisions, and with what support or standing. A new artifact derived from three sources is not a fourth independent confirmation of them. Later inquiry can inspect, reuse, dispute or revise it.

Hiding an overlay is a view choice. Retracting a relation, revising a constellation, making a variant and editing source are different authored operations. Source changes should identify affected bindings and preserve unaffected composition, not recreate the whole field or silently force old interpretations onto new text.

## 6. Three distinctions which must not collapse

**Source field / constellation organisation** distinguishes kinds of knowledge authorship. **3:3 Expressions / 4:2 Technè** distinguishes operating readings of the same field. **Native authoritative work / retained kernel resources / presentation** distinguishes engineering responsibilities. None is a substitute name for another.

Ordinary Wiki work remains useful without QL. Technè is itself creative and operates through Expressions. Both application readings can expose source material and constructed constellations. The kernel supports fast continuity across them without becoming a new semantic owner.

## 8. Complete intended experience

Open an ordinary linked Markdown project without QL preparation. Read and follow its actual links, backlinks and tags. Select exact passages; initiate a frame-first or material-first constellation; work with Epii on the shared construction; manipulate its glyphs, relations, text and media in the real Expression system. Save it, compose Scenes and a Palace, return a new artifact and deliberate QL relations, and find them again through the Wiki. Reopen after switching or restarting, change a source, and review the affected bindings without losing unrelated work.

This is the intended creative practice. The specification makes its contracts concrete; the Wayfinder assigns implementation; the UX spine retains what the person and agent must actually be able to do.
