## 0. The figure, stated once

Hesse's Game is "a mode of playing with the total contents and values of our
culture": players at a fixed instrument — the organ — drawing canonical
symbols from an Archive that admits them "scarcely ever," developing themes
the way a fugue develops a subject, before a congregation whose meditation
is "the main thing," under a discipline of days, with every game recorded
and every master free to resign. Two schools of play: the **formal**, whose
unity comes from the contents themselves, and the **psychological**, whose
unity is achieved in prescribed meditations that guide the player toward
experiencing the whole.

The Digital Logos Workstation is that mode of play aimed at a life: a mode
of playing with the total contents and values of a *world* — ground, work,
form, knowing — where the instrument is real (a transport, a rack, four
projections, a spine), the symbols are admitted through neutral doors by
their native owners, the meditation is evidence law (nothing counts without
executed proof; every modulated value shows its modulator), and the players
are not members of one order but **worlds themselves**. Castalia had one
Game and an Order; the DLW has as many worlds as players, and the shared
field is their Players' Village.

The figure earns its place by being *load-bearing*: at every point below,
the Game names something the design also names, and the overlap is where
the intent is clearest.

**The figure's referent** (owner clarification, corrected 2026-10-11).
Epi-Logos is a **philosophical project** — not a feature, not "the playing"
in the abstract. Its authored body is the corpus: the Antykathera essay
(*The Return of Zero*, Objective Internality); Quaternal Logic/MEF as its
formal field; **Bimba**, the archetypal map of which software is the
*pratibimba* — the reflection that returns what the originating form means
when made operative ("QL as bimba, software as pratibimba,"
FOUNDING-POSITIONS §4′) — with its M/M′ determinations and coordinate
journeys (the L5 journey carries Vāk's graduated speech — Parā, Pasyantī,
Madhyamā, Vaikharī — Abheda/Bhedābheda/Bheda, pratyabhijñā); the Ta-Onta
agent-world; Nara's dialogical presence; Epii's depth; held together by the
Covenant work (Whole / Mark / Aim). The system — the six products, the
substrate, the DLW shell — is deliberately **paradigm-neutral exoteric
machinery**: "the substrate must not presuppose the Epi interpretation
which will later operate through it" (EXPRESSION-WORLD-SUBSTRATE-WAYFINDER
§0). **Epi-logos mode is the projection of the esoteric identity over the
exoteric identity of the system** — bimba over pratibimba, made into the
shell; `WorkspaceMode "epi-logos"` ("The authored world: the essay, Bimba,
the Epii material, the products") is that projection's current seat. When
the instruments perform Epi-Logos — a lens resolving a Bimba coordinate, a
scene carrying Vāk's densification, a companion holding Nara's presence —
that performance is the **esoteric form of the thing**, not its
illustration. And the corpus is a double game: it is the owner's own Glass
Bead Game — themes developed and dismissed across years — and it is the
*native game of the system*: what the DLW exists to let any player play.
The figure closes on itself: per the Covenant's own vocabulary, a Figure of
the Mark "can disclose a Whole through metaphor, historical precedent,
exemplary object, image, practice… while retaining ILLUMINATES and BOUNDARY
together" — which is exactly what this study is. In reading the system
through Hesse, the system's own practice was already being enacted.

## 1. The fixed instrument

One World, four projections, real panes, one rack; one transport wearing
five meanings; the encounter as the only transition. This is what every
player touches, and it is deliberately a *closed, disciplined* form:

- **The World** is never rendered directly — it is always encountered
  through a projection: **Earth** (space), **Timeline** (time),
  **Constellation** (relation), **Expressions** (form). Graph and agent
  inspection are implicit, callable from anywhere, never competing modes.
- **The rack** is the plugin system of the whole environment: devices attach
  to the focused pane's encounter, each with one parameter model and three
  presentations (compact face, expanded pool, full pane). Families are
  carved by native owners through a neutral admission door — "a seventh
  product should integrate with no core change."
- **The transport** keeps its slots in the same places in every mode; only
  their meaning changes. That is the single deepest act of the design: the
  form is fixed so the contents can vary without relearning.
- **The time spine** is locked at the top — chronos, civil time, cron,
  transport, occasion — so that "an LFO was always time; this world's LFOs
  are transits, day phases, cron cadences and clock phases."
- **Modulation** is typed routing with the honesty law: *every modulated
  parameter shows its modulator; nothing moves without a visible cause.*
  This is the Game's meditation clause turned into interface physics —
  Castalia's failure was invisible authority; the DLW colours the modulated
  knob.

The Game-figure: Lusor Basiliensis's language, "in which mathematics and
music played an equal part" — and beneath it, the deeper rhyme. The shell's
first law (one World, many projections; nothing copied into view-specific
models) is *the same shape* as the musical system's first law (M1 is the
oscillator source; sound and visible form are projections of one evolving
state, "never effects sharing a timestamp"). The DLW's ontology and its
acoustics are one ontology. Hesse made music the model of the universal
language; this system makes the universal language literally musical.

## 2. Movement I — Central: the ground

**What it is for:** creating, exploring, sharing — the private game, the
study, the day.

**The session.** The day opens (08:30 — the transport's punch row is the
civil law sitting in the shell's top bar). The Base arrangement is Central's
ground: the left browser holds **Days, Flows, Beings, Things, Goals; Files,
Wiki; New (exactly Day · Flow · Beings · Things)**; the centre is the
documents themselves, paper-toned; the right is the situated agent
(Conversation · Activity · Context · Inspect); the detail row is document
devices. Reading is one locus (UX1): page position, contents, graph,
explorer and address coordinated; a tangent opens in preview without losing
the main inquiry; keep, replace, promote and return are distinct acts
(UX2). Click selects, double-click opens, drag moves (UX3). Graph and
connections share one neighbourhood (UX4).

The forms are the Game's *characters*, typed: the **Day die** — a situated
six-office whole (4+2), the die face turned by ‹ ›; the **Flow** — Dialogue
· Flow · Journal with *declared participants*, a person drawn solid, an
agent dissolving into points; **Beings** — "a presence and its world";
**Things** — the same carrier for works and collections, with bindings
(world, subject, C-grammar office, sources) that keep every page a citizen
of the ground rather than a floating page.

Working with the agent is one continuous gesture: **Highlight · Note · To
packet · Answer this**. The highlight is "an observation, never an offset
into the backing HTML" — ranges kept with node, source and revision; the
packet is shown as *exactly what the agent receives*, the companion's
Context tab shows the same packet, and **Answer** — Base's transport ▶ —
sends it. What comes back returns as attributable work, not as chat
weather.

Sharing is projection, never copy: a deliberate
`central-wiki-selection` → `world-presentation` → `projection` pipeline with
sentinel tests proving private ground never enters a projection; page
authoring flows READ→AUTHOR→PREVIEW→ratified next revision, because
"working state is not public state" and "presentation refinement ≠
source-return proposal ≠ accepted native source mutation." The wiki itself
is never edited directly — knowledge travels as returns, with provenance,
through the native owner.

**The figure.** "To this day everyone is free to play the Game privately."
Central is the private game and its discipline: the day as a performed
score that *ends* (close routine, 21:00), the forms as the repertoire, the
packet as the summons (*Berufung* — "a summons comes from without"), the
Answer as the game's first ▶. And the civil law is the Game's festival
discipline domesticated: Hesse's players lived under "precepts which even
govern the length of time they are allowed to sleep"; here the precept is
the day itself, opening and closing on the transport.

## 3. Movement II — Factory: work as World phenomena

**What it is for:** development — commissioning, watching, steering,
receiving — where the work itself becomes material of the same world.

**The session.** A run begins as a human sentence — the commission's
*purpose*; card titles are purposes, never refs. The Desk board shows NEEDS
YOU / ACTIVE / QUEUED / RECENT, unit segments shaded by leg standing,
"never a percentage" — standing, not progress-bar theatre. The Run page is
the performed game in four readings: **Map** (one lane per work unit, forks,
convergence joins, gate bars, the frontier outlined), **Trajectory** (the
journal replayed — Input/Model/Tools as a lane strip, CONTEXT/USER/ASSISTANT/
THINK/TOOL rows with call and result joined by id, footer stats only where
the harness actually supplies them), **Live** (who carries it now, on which
workcell, on what Git basis), **Handoff** (the outcome, the diff labelled
committed/staged/working, verification with its staleness named, remaining
work, a copyable continuation). Tasks are the conversation; the composer is
model-first with permission modes. Custody is visible: the hold/hand-back
light — "you edited HeroShot physics while Honey held it; Honey's hold is
suspended." The detail row is the **agent chain device**:
gateway → agent → skills → world → git.

**The figure.** The Games at Waldzell were cooperative constructions under a
leader — no opponents, no score; the Factory run is the same form aimed at
work: a leader (the agent), a score (the commission), a performed sequence
(the trajectory), a record (the handoff), an audience (the owner) who
*recognises* what came back. Custody is the Game's office-law inverted into
instrument design — "the higher the office, the tighter the bondage" becomes
power that always names its holder and hands back. And the deepest
continuity: *Magister Ludi* "originally meant… simply schoolmaster" — the
Factory's output is not artifacts but the owner's growing capacity to
commission, read trajectories, and judge returns. The runs are lessons; the
Desk is the schoolroom.

**Verification.** The **Factory vertical** (§17.1) is the designated first
proof: a real Factory agent performs a bounded piece of work on a real World
object; the result is recovered through Earth, Timeline, Constellation and
Expressions — identity, provenance, work and context continuous throughout;
repeated with views closed, reopening, occupant change and a retained
occasion. (The joins are named — runs as Timeline tracks, results as
Constellation nodes — and the recorded `FactoryLive` producer-consumer fault
is the named repair.)

## 4. Movement III — Expressions: the sounding and forming surface

**What it is for:** scene creation — the physical (and musical) sonal
surface; the full suites: musical development, physics simulation,
glyph/symbol sequential editing with text. The 3:3 reading's body.

**The session** (Appendix A carries the full step-by-step). The centre pane
is the living field — the retained application doing its physics at 60 fps
while the shell holds instrument chrome around it. The transport reads like
an instrument because it is one: **Time Scale** streams a live transient
into the frame at 20 Hz (the clock visibly slows; nothing writes; release
commits one history entry); **Arm** decides what Play does; the record dot
takes a **property take** — record, replace, or append "next section" — and
the sampler reads the live override, so a curve recorded during a drag holds
the ramp, not a staircase; **Pin mode** (Ableton's key-map analogue) pins
any control to any object with a Field | Follow | Bind destination and a
Shared | Local scope; the **engine light** reads Recording > Held > Running;
the **pinned strip** carries the owner's seed slots (Viscosity, Shape hold —
a rack macro over snapRigidity+densityTether, Particle size, Cymatic with
Plate Size, Relational, Turbulence, Ink Opacity).

The detail row is **Scene | Studio**: the Scene editor (duration, transition,
Capture, Save & next, snapshots, loop, reorder as one native request), the
States editor (glyph formation steps — text/glyph/image/ASCII sources with
masks, Sobel and threshold, template glyphs ring/disc/triangle/plane/yantra/
cymatic, per-step overrides of force, scale, tint, rotation, position,
layers; fold a state into an earlier formation), Takes (envelopes drawn,
previewed, removable). The device pool admits 22 declared families — field
(physics, pointer, relational, medium, contacts, morph, focus, colour, ink,
depth, resonance), entity (formation, force, sound, meaning), scene
(automation, scene, text, body, blueprint, arrange) — each face honest by
construction: "writePath ONLY where a real owner write exists; a row
without a writePath IS a reading — that is the grammar."

**The physics.** A GPGPU particle field with per-entity forces
(attract/repel/vortex), relational forces (orbital, N-body, chaotic),
collisions, a shared medium — and the **cymatic resonator**: a
continuously-driven damped modal resonator, 2D the free square Chladni plate
(64 modes, Kirchhoff eigenfrequencies), 3D a standing-wave cavity, a point
drive coupling by the mode's own amplitude, every mode a live damped
oscillator — "nothing is ever reset, reseeded, or swapped for a stored
picture." Seven physical anchors are the strongest distinct eigenmodes,
which semantic systems may interpret but the physics does not.

**The sound.** Every entity may carry a small voice — frequency, waveform,
gain, attack, release, pan — and `followCymatic` sounds the entity's own
cymatic frequency: **the pitch rides the plate**. The M1 instrument (the
torus/field owner) sits in the rack at its declared presentation scale with
sky epochs and owner standing, its meters measuring scheduling and pointedly
offering *no strike* — performance input belongs to #281's packets, and no
sounding claim is made before its owner lands it. The track adapters encode
the discipline: the audio material's time base *is* the owner's 48 kHz
sample clock (any other rate refused — no implicit resampling); the physics
track encodes common-cause; the visual-only glyph is "honestly labelled so
it is never certified as physical synthesis."

**Scenes carry text.** Page-text blocks with roles (caption, kicker),
entities with roles (lead, goal, participants) and text glyphs (◯ ◇ ▽); the
day record shows the pattern executed: a curated single-voice explanation
scene, a two-voice claim/verdict variation created only because the roles
were missing, then reselected from the material list. The reuse law is the
Game's canon made small: *choose first, create only when missing, reselect
the saved form.*

**The figure.** This is Hesse's **psychological school** made flesh — games
whose unity is achieved in prescribed meditations, guided stage by stage.
A scene is a guided contemplation of a configuration: entities held in
formation, a duration, a transition, a text that names what is being
contemplated. And the resonator is the Game's origin literally rebuilt:
Hesse's Game descended from music — the fugue as the discipline of
development — and here *sound and visible form are projections of one
evolving state*, the sand gathering in the nodal lines while the entity
sings its own frequency. The Game's deepest claim, that one language underlies
music, mathematics and everything else, is not asserted anywhere in this
system more plainly than in `cymaticResonator.ts`. The take is the recorded
performance; the pin is the player's registration of stops; the seed strip
is the organ's fixed roster; and the strike withheld from the M1 face is
the discipline of the conservatoire — technique admitted, performance
earned.

## 8. The verification layer: the praxis is the spec

The owner's framing, made precise: **the vision of intended use is the true
spec and verification layer.** Concretely:

1. **A capability exists when the session it belongs to can be performed
   end-to-end against real owners** — identity, provenance and standing
   continuous — not when its manifest declares it. The C0–C5 ladder already
   encodes this; the TD cases and the two verticals are its spine; the walk
   artifacts (37/37 refinement, 63/63 §9, FE/GE/RE/CO/CT) are its executed
   pattern. The sessions documented in §2–§6 are the acceptance scenarios
   the shell convergence is building towards; a UI change is admissible
   exactly when these sessions survive it (domain invariance, one-field
   identity).
2. **The invariants any change must preserve are praxis sentences, not
   type signatures:** a highlight is an observation, not an offset; a drawn
   relation is a proposal, not a mutation; a projection never carries
   private ground; an epistemic standing is never upgraded by placement;
   every moved value names its cause; a refusal mutates nothing; a day
   ends.
3. **The Game's warnings become session-shaped tests.** *Rococo:* a frame
   whose beauty exceeds its landed session is glass — the test is the arc
   of §7: perform it; whatever cannot carry its step is ornament, downgraded
   by the same evidence law that downgrades a contradicted DSP claim.
   *Oracle:* any device that judges synthesis for the player — scores the
   scene, picks the reading — breaks the meditation clause; the modulated
   knob must show its cause and the Magister must remain a person.
   *Sterility:* Plinio's charge — analysis without new music — is checked
   by the ratio of derivations to landed voice: the Live corpus serves the
   World's own sounding instrument (#281) or it is the conservatoire
   outliving the orchestra.
4. **The measure of the whole is the leaving.** The workstation is working
   when its players leave it with more world than they brought: days closed
   into wiki returns, runs landed, expressions published, readers served by
   the site alone, a pupil who no longer needs the teacher. The Game's
   biography ends with a schoolmaster walking down a mountain; the proving
   matrix should have a row that counts the climbing-down.
